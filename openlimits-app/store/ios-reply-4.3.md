What is different from other Screen Time apps
Most screen-time apps limit minutes or block apps on a schedule. Open Limits counts how many times a day an app is opened, which is a different habit to change:
- A daily number of opens per app or per group of apps ("Instagram 5 opens a day"). The shield shows "open 3 of 5 today" and locks the app for the rest of the day when the opens are used up.
- One open lasts the number of minutes the person chose (15 to 60), so switching back to the app during that time does not use another open.
- Wait-before-open: the shield asks you to wait a few seconds before the open counts, so each open is a decision.
- Nudge messages: the person writes their own message for each app (for example "Did you finish your homework?"), shown on the shield and played as a notification sound, either read aloud by the device's voice or in their own recorded voice.
- An emergency "hold 5 seconds to open anyway" that counts as an extra open, and strict mode (no "open anyway", a 30-second wait before a limit can be loosened), streaks and a 7-day chart of opens.
- No account, no ads, no tracking; nothing leaves the phone.
