## Description (both stores)

MES AI is a free AI chat and picture maker for people with a Hive account.

Log in with your Hive account through the Hive Keychain app and get, every day, for free:
• 10 AI chat messages with fast open models (DeepSeek, Qwen or Llama): questions, explanations, writing, code, maths.
• 3 AI pictures made from your words.

No subscription, no payment, no ads. Your Hive keys stay in the Keychain app: logging in uses HiveAuth, so MES never sees them. The free messages come back every day at 00:00 UTC.

Chats and pictures are kept only on your phone. Save a chat as a file, share it, or back it up to Hive (encrypted) if you want to keep it.

Don't have a Hive account yet? Get one free at signup.hive.io and install the Hive Keychain app.

Made by Math Easy Solutions (mes.fm). The same chat runs in any browser at mes.fm/ai.
