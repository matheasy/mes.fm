    About MES Site Search

    • Searches the titles, descriptions and sections of every page on mes.fm: the calculators and tools, math tutorials and Math Q/A livestreams, memes, quotes and dream homes, puzzles, and the 9/11, Hutchison Effect, science and crypto posts.
    • Every word has to match (the start of a word is enough, so calc finds calculator). If no page has all of them, the closest matches are shown instead.
    • Pick a section above to narrow the results, or pick one with an empty search box to browse everything in it.
    • On any mes.fm page, the magnifier button next to A-/A+ (or the / key, or Ctrl/Cmd+K) opens a quick search.
    • Results are shareable: the address bar keeps your search, e.g. mes.fm/search?q=cubic+formula. The search runs in your browser; what you type isn't sent anywhere.
