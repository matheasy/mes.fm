Before you accuse anyone

  • Text detectors are wrong often enough that OpenAI withdrew its own in 2023. They flag non-native English writers and very formal writing far more than others.
  • Ask for drafts, version history (Google Docs and Word keep it) or a short chat about the work: much better evidence than any score.
  • Open long documents in the Text Viewer or the Word Viewer, then paste the part you want checked.

AI detector for pictures, videos and text

Was this picture made with AI? Was this essay written by ChatGPT? MES AI Detector looks for the evidence instead of pretending to know: what the file itself says about how it was made, what a small on-device model thinks, and how the text compares with typical chatbot writing. Each clue is marked as pointing to AI, to a real camera or person, or neither, and how strong it is.

What it checks

  • Content Credentials (C2PA): the signed “nutrition label” that Adobe Firefly, ChatGPT, Microsoft, Google, Sora, Leica, Sony, Nikon and Samsung phones attach.
  • IPTC digital source type: the standard field that says “made by a trained algorithm”, “edited with generative AI” or “captured by a camera”.
  • Generator notes: Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, NovelAI, InvokeAI and Fooocus save the prompt and settings inside the PNG; Midjourney saves its job ID.
  • Camera data: make, model, exposure and GPS from the EXIF (a hint toward a real photo, but it can be copied).
  • Error level and noise views: pictures that show how evenly the file compresses and how the sensor noise is spread. Pasted-in or generated areas sometimes stand out.
  • Whole posts from a link: paste a Hive post or comment (PeakD, InLeo, Ecency, 3Speak), a web article or a YouTube link and every picture, the video and the text are lined up for checking, with searches for copies of the pictures and of the most distinctive sentences.
  • Text statistics: sentence-length variety, range of words, words chatbots overuse, leftovers such as “Certainly! Here is”, and an optional predictability score from a small language model.

Questions

Can any tool prove a picture or text was made by AI?

Only when the file says so itself (Content Credentials or generator notes), and even those can be removed or, without a signature check, faked. Everything else is a probability. A missing label proves nothing: screenshots, social sites and messaging apps remove metadata.

Why not just give a percentage?

Because the percentages other sites show are not as precise as they look. The same text can score 10% on one site and 90% on another. Showing the clues lets you see how much weight they deserve.

Can it tell if a picture or an article was copied?

It gives you the leads: for each picture, one-click searches on Google Lens, TinEye, Yandex and Bing (TinEye's "Oldest" sort is the best way to find the first copy), the uploader that PeakD records in its picture links, and searches for the same Hive upload in other posts; for the text, exact-phrase searches for its most distinctive sentences. No free tool can search every post and picture, so a search that finds nothing is not proof the work is original.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Files and text are read by your browser. The optional models are downloaded from Hugging Face once and run on your device. Only if you choose “Ask MES AI” does the text go to MES AI, which you pay for per message.
