What is an RSS feed?

An RSS feed is a small file that lists a site's newest items with their titles, links and dates. A feed reader checks it for you, so you see new posts from every site you follow in one place, in order, with nothing filtered out.

How do I follow MES with RSS?

Press Copy link on a feed and paste it into your feed reader's "Add feed" or "Subscribe" box, or press Feedly or Inoreader to subscribe there in one click. Everything new has all of it; the topic feeds follow just one hub.

What is Podping?

Podping is an open way to say "this feed just changed" on the Hive blockchain, made by the Podcasting 2.0 project. Instead of checking every feed every hour, apps watch Hive and fetch a feed only when it is announced. It is free and nobody owns it.

Can I play these in a podcast app?

Not yet: the feeds link to pages and videos but carry no audio files, so podcast apps can list them but cannot play them. They work in every RSS reader.
