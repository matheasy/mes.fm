For grown-ups

  • Big cards, spoken words. Every card says its word out loud. Tap the picture to hear it again, swipe (or use the arrow keys) for the next one.
  • "What's this?" The page says a word and your child picks the right picture from 2, 3 or 4. Wrong taps just say what that picture is and try again: there is no losing.
  • Settings are hidden from little fingers: hold the ⚙️ Grown-ups button for 2 seconds to choose categories, letter case, show or hide the word, the voice, speed and pitch.
  • Kid-proof screen: Fill window or Full screen hides everything else; to leave, hold ✕ for 2 seconds.
  • No ads, no sign-up, nothing uploaded. Settings stay in this browser. The voice is your device's own speech voice.
