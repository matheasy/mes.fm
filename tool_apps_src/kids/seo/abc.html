ABC flashcards that say the letter

A to Z, one big letter at a time, with a picture and its word: "A. A is for apple." Cards go in order by default (or shuffled), and on a keyboard, pressing a letter jumps to that letter's card, so even key-bashing teaches something.

What's this? says "Find the letter B!" and your child picks the right letter out of two, three or four. Grown-ups (hold ⚙️ for 2 seconds) can show capital and small letters together (Aa), capitals only (A) or small letters only (a).

Questions

Does it say the letter name or the sound?
The letter name ("A, A is for apple"). The example word gives the usual sound.
What's X for?
Xylophone, drawn on the page.
