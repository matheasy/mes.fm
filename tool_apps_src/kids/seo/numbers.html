Counting for toddlers: numbers 1 to 10 (or 20)

Each card shows a number big and the same number of pictures to count, and the voice says the number and counts out loud: "Three. One, two, three!" Cards go in order by default; pressing a digit on a keyboard jumps to that number (0 is ten).

What's this? says "Find four!" and your child picks the right number. Grown-ups (hold ⚙️ for 2 seconds) can choose numbers up to 5, 10 or 20 and switch the counting off.
