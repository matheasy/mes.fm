Animal sounds for kids, with real recordings

Tap through 33 animals: each card shows the animal big, says its name, then plays a real recording of the animal: a dog barking, a cat meowing, a rooster crowing, a lion roaring, an elephant trumpeting, a whale singing and more. Three animals (cow, tiger and mouse) have no free recording we could use, so the voice says their sound ("Moo!") instead. Press Animal sound to hear just the sound again.

What's this? plays the sound and asks "Who says moo? Find the cow!" and your child taps the right animal out of two, three or four.

Questions

Where do the sounds come from?
From Wikimedia Commons. Every recording is in the public domain or under CC0, and each one is listed with its author and licence under "Animal sound credits" below the cards. We trimmed them to a few seconds and evened out the volume.
Can I turn the recordings off?
Yes: hold ⚙️ Grown-ups for 2 seconds and untick "Play real animal recordings"; the voice then says the sound word ("Woof woof") instead.
Are there ads?
No. MES Kids pages are ad-free.
