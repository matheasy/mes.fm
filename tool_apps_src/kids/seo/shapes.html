Shapes and colours for toddlers

Twelve shapes (circle, square, triangle, rectangle, oval, star, heart, diamond, pentagon, hexagon, crescent and arrow) and eleven colours (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, brown, black, white and grey), drawn big and bright and said out loud. Swipe or tap the arrows for the next one, tap the picture to hear it again.

What's this? asks "Find the star!" or "Find blue!" and your child taps the right picture. Grown-ups can switch shapes or colours off (hold ⚙️ for 2 seconds).
