Math games for toddlers and young kids

Pick an age at the top. Each question is said out loud and shown with big pictures; your child taps the answer. A wrong tap just explains and lets them try again: there is no losing, and every right answer earns a star.

Ages 2-3
Count up to five things ("How many ducks?") and "Which group has more?"
Ages 4-5
Count up to ten, add with pictures (2 apples and 3 apples), take away (5 balloons, 2 fly away), and which number comes before or after.
Ages 6-7
Adding and taking away up to 20, counting by twos, fives and tens, "what comes next?" shape patterns, and how many sides a shape has.

Tips: count out loud together, point at each picture, and use real things (spoons, blocks) for the same questions. Grown-ups can choose 2, 3 or 4 answers to pick from (hold ⚙️ for 2 seconds). Fill window or Full screen keeps little fingers on the game; hold ✕ for 2 seconds to leave. Link straight to an age with ?age=1, ?age=2 or ?age=3.
