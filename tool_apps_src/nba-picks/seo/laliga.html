Track your La Liga table prediction against the live standings

MES La Liga Picks checks your predicted final table of the 20 La Liga clubs against the current standings: how many clubs are in the exact place, within one, two or three places, in the right zone (top 4 Champions League, 5th to 7th the other European places, mid-table, 18th to 20th relegation), plus points, closeness and rank correlation, with a picture that joins each pick to where the club is now.

How the scores work

Exact counts the clubs in the place you picked; within 1, 2 or 3 the clubs that close; right zone the clubs in the zone you put them in; right side of the line whether you had a club in the top 4 or not; points 3 for an exact place, 2 for one off, 1 for two off. Clubs level on points, goal difference and goals scored after the same number of games share their places. The European places are a guide: Spain sometimes gets a fifth Champions League place, and cup winners shift the others.

Questions

Why are some clubs not in my old list?

Three clubs are relegated and three promoted every summer. A list saved for last season keeps the clubs that are still in La Liga, in your order, and adds the new ones at the bottom for you to place.

Where does the table come from?

From ESPN’s public standings feed, refreshed about every 10 minutes.

Is anything about me stored?

Your picks stay in this browser. Only if you post to the leaderboard are the name you chose and your table stored, with a private key in your browser that lets you change or remove it.
