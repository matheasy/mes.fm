Track your MLB standings and World Series predictions against the live table

MES MLB Picks checks your predicted order of the American League and National League (15 teams each: 1-3 division winners, 4-6 wild cards), the six divisions and the whole league (1 to 30) against the current MLB standings, and scores your postseason picks (Division Series, League Championship Series, World Series and champion) as the series are played.

How the scores work

Exact, within 1, 2 or 3 spots, right tier (division winner, wild card, out), right side of the line (in or out of the 6 playoff spots), points (3 exact, 2 one off, 1 two off), closeness and Spearman’s ρ. Postseason picks earn 1 point for each Division Series team you got right, 2 for each LCS team, 4 for each World Series team and 8 for the champion.

Questions

What happens when the season is over?

After game 162 the table is final: your picks are scored against it and the leaderboard closes. The postseason tab keeps scoring your bracket until the World Series is won. You can already make and save picks for next season; they are scored once its first games are played.

Where do the standings come from?

From ESPN’s public standings and scoreboard feeds, refreshed about every 10 minutes, in ESPN’s published seeding.

Is anything about me stored?

Your picks stay in this browser. Only if you post to the leaderboard are the name you chose and your picks stored, with a private key in your browser that lets you change or remove them.
