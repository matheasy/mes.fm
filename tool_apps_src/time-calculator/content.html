Minutes to decimal hours (for payroll)

How to calculate the hours between two times

Take the end time minus the start time. If the end is earlier than the start, the shift runs past midnight, so add 24 hours. Then take off any unpaid break. Example: 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes; minus a 30-minute lunch that is 8:00, or 8.00 decimal hours. A night shift from 10:00 PM to 6:30 AM is 8 hours 30 minutes.

Hours and minutes vs decimal hours

Payroll systems usually want decimal hours: minutes divided by 60. So 7 hours 45 minutes is 7 + 45/60 = 7.75 hours, and 7.75 hours back to clock form is 7:45, not 7:75. Common values: 15 min = 0.25 h, 20 min = 0.33 h, 30 min = 0.5 h, 45 min = 0.75 h. Multiply decimal hours by the hourly rate to get pay: 7.75 × $20 = $155.

Adding and subtracting durations

Add seconds to seconds, minutes to minutes and hours to hours, then carry every 60 seconds into a minute and every 60 minutes into an hour. 2:45:30 + 1:20:45: 30 + 45 = 75 s = 1 min 15 s; 45 + 20 + 1 = 66 min = 1 h 6 min; 2 + 1 + 1 = 4 h, so the answer is 4:06:15.

Overtime

In the US the federal rule is overtime after 40 hours in a work week, at 1.5 times the regular rate; a few states (California, for one) also count overtime after 8 hours in a day. Canadian provinces typically use 8 hours a day or 40-44 a week. This page lets you switch each rule on and change the numbers, and never counts the same hour twice.

Questions

How many hours is 8 AM to 5 PM?
9 hours; with a 1-hour unpaid lunch, 8 hours.
How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?
Divide by 60. 40 minutes = 40 ÷ 60 = 0.67 hours. The table on the Convert tab lists every 5 minutes.
Can I enter times in 24-hour format?
Yes. Every time box takes either style (18:30 or 6:30 pm); the small 12h / 24h switch inside the box changes how times are shown on every MES page.
What does "round to the nearest 15 minutes" do?
It rounds each day's worked time, so 7:53 becomes 8:00 and 7:52 becomes 7:45. Some employers round punches this way; others pay to the minute.
Is my timesheet stored anywhere?
Only in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded. Copy it as a table to paste into a spreadsheet or email.
