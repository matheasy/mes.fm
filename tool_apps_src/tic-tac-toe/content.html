    Tic-tac-toe with fifteen ways to play

    Play classic noughts and crosses against the computer or a friend, then try the variants: misère (where three in a row loses), wild tic-tac-toe, numerical tic-tac-toe, bigger boards with four or five in a row, gomoku, a gravity game where pieces drop, ultimate tic-tac-toe, 3D Qubic, Notakto and Order & Chaos. You can also make your own board: any size from 3 × 3 to 19 × 19, up to four layers, three to seven in a row. Nothing to install and no account.

    How strong is the computer?

    Easy
    Mostly plays at random, and only sometimes notices that it can win. Good for young children.
    Medium
    Always takes a win and always stops yours when it can, but otherwise often plays a harmless move instead of the best one.
    Hard
    On small boards it works out the game to the end, so on the classic 3 × 3 board it never loses. On bigger boards it searches several moves ahead and weighs every possible line; on ultimate tic-tac-toe, Notakto and Order & Chaos it plays out thousands of random games from each possible move and picks the one that wins most often. It thinks in the background, so the page never freezes.

    Questions

    Can you always win at tic-tac-toe?
    No. If both players make no mistakes the classic game is always a draw. You can only win when the other player slips, which is why the Hard computer never loses it. Bigger boards are different: on 4 × 4 with three in a row, the first player can always force a win.
    What is the best first move?
    On the classic board the centre and the corners are both fine; an edge is the riskiest start but still a draw with good play. Against a beginner, a corner gives them the most ways to go wrong.
    How does online play work?
    Make a game, send the link, and the board updates for both of you within a second or two. Moves are checked on mes.fm's server so nobody can cheat or play out of turn. If the other player disappears for three minutes on their turn you can claim the win.
    Is there an undo?
    Yes, against the computer and against a friend on the same device (not in online games). The Hint button shows the Hard computer's choice for your move.
