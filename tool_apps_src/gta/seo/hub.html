Questions

When did GTA 6 come out?
Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Digital preloading opened on November 10.
What time did GTA 6 unlock?
Rockstar said midnight local time in every region, so New Zealand was first and Hawaii last. The PlayStation Store put the USA and Canada on midnight Eastern (9 p.m. on the 18th on the West Coast), and Xbox Cloud Gaming started at 1 a.m. Eastern.
How big is GTA 6?
Up to 212 GB depending on the console, as Rockstar said on November 3, plus two small updates released before launch. Leave extra free space for later updates and saves.
Is GTA 6 on PC?
Not at launch: it came out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and no PC version has been announced.
Is this an official GTA site?
No. MES is not connected with Rockstar Games or Take-Two. Everything here is original and made by fans of the series.
