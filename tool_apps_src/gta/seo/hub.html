The MES GTA 6 Hub

Free, unofficial tools for the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI: a countdown to the exact moment it unlocks where you live, a download time and storage calculator for the roughly 212 GB install, and a stunt jump simulator with real car physics. The page also collects the latest GTA headlines from gaming news sites.

Questions

When does GTA 6 come out?
Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Digital preloading opens on November 10.
What time does GTA 6 unlock?
Rockstar has only given the date. PlayStation usually unlocks digital games at midnight in each region, and Xbox has used a single worldwide time. The countdown page shows midnight in your time zone and the first unlock in the world (New Zealand).
How big is GTA 6?
About 212 GB, as Rockstar said on November 3. A day-one patch could add more, and you need extra free space for updates and saves.
Is GTA 6 on PC?
Not at launch. Only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S have been announced.
Is this an official GTA site?
No. MES is not connected with Rockstar Games or Take-Two. Everything here is original and made by fans of the series.
