A GTA 6 map made by players

This is a shared GTA 6 map where anyone can drop a pin on something worth finding: a secret, a rare animal, a good fishing spot, an Easter egg, a collectible, a stunt jump or just a great view. Everyone else can vote each pin real or fake, so the finds that check out rise to the top and the made-up ones sink and disappear.

How to add a pin

Choose Add a secret (or click any empty spot on the map), click where the thing is, pick what kind of thing it is, give it a short title and say how to find it. You can add a YouTube clip that shows it. Your pin appears for everyone right away.

Questions

Is this the real GTA 6 map?
Not yet. Until the game comes out on November 19, 2026, the picture is a painted fan map of Leonida (made-up roads and towns) using the region names Rockstar has published (Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga). On launch day it will be fully redrawn from the game with real streets, highways and deep zoom, and pins move over with it.
When is a pin "Verified"?
When it has at least 5 more up votes than down votes and at least three out of four votes are up. A pin that falls to −5 is hidden.
Do I need an account?
No. Your browser remembers your votes and the pins you added, so you can change a vote or delete your own pin later from the same browser.
Can I post links?
Only a YouTube clip in its own box. Other links are blocked to keep spam off the map. Use Report on anything rude or nonsense: pins reported from several networks are hidden.
