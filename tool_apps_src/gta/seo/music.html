Music for GTA fans

A place to listen to GTA-style music while you play, drive or stream. The top of the list is Grand Theft Panda: Vice City Radio, a 35-track album that the label Panda Beats (Raven on Hive) made for the release of GTA 6: hip hop, Vice City beats, synthwave, synthpop and 80s rock, the same mix of styles as the radio stations in the game. Two more Panda Beats albums fit the mood: Night Ride (dark synthwave for night drives) and Chill-House Moments (sunny chill house).

Questions

Can I use this music in my own streams and videos?
The Panda Beats albums are made for streamers and are copyright free: Panda Beats says they are never claimed on YouTube or Twitch. Check pandabeatsmusic.com for their exact terms. The songs on the GTA radio stations and in the trailers are licensed music: fine to listen to, not free to use in your own videos.
Why 3Speak?
3Speak is a video site on the Hive blockchain, where Raven posts each album. You can also play the albums on YouTube or Spotify with the switch under the player.
What songs are in the GTA VI trailers?
Trailer 1 (December 2023) uses Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road". Trailer 2 (May 2025) uses The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together".
What are the GTA 6 radio stations?
Rockstar has not listed them yet. They will be added here once the game is out on November 19, 2026.
Why do the station names open YouTube searches?
The full radio recordings on YouTube are uploaded by fans, not by Rockstar, so this page links to them instead of playing them here.
