When GTA 6 unlocked around the world

GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026. This page counts how long it has been out where you are, from midnight in your own time zone (your browser tells the page; nothing is sent anywhere). Switch to First in the world to count from midnight in New Zealand, where it unlocked first, or pick any city. The table shows when midnight arrived in 18 cities and how long ago that was.

Questions

What time did GTA 6 unlock?
Rockstar said midnight local time in all regions. PlayStation players in the USA and Canada got it at midnight Eastern (9 p.m. on November 18 on the West Coast), as the PlayStation Store listing said, and Xbox Cloud Gaming began at 1 a.m. Eastern.
Where did it come out first and last?
New Zealand first, then Australia and Asia, Europe, the Americas, and Hawaii last, 23 hours after New Zealand.
Is there a map?
Rockstar released a free printable map of Leonida on its official site on launch day. The MES GTA 6 map is our own players' map where you can pin secrets and finds.
Can I put it on a big screen?
Yes: Fill window (W) makes the counter fill the browser window and Full screen (F) fills the whole screen.
