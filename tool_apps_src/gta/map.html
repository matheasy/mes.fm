      About this map

      The picture is a rough fan sketch of Leonida made from the region names Rockstar has published. It is not the game's map and the places are approximate. Once GTA 6 is out (November 19, 2026) it will be redrawn from the real game, and every pin keeps its region, so nothing gets lost.

      Your own map, free: tick Found on any pin to track your progress (Hide found and Next not found help you sweep a region), add a private note to any pin, or drop private markers only you can see. No account and no limit; it is all kept in this browser, with a backup file to move it.

      Known places (the pins with a yellow ◆) are a starter guide written by MES from the trailers and Rockstar’s region descriptions; they are approximate until the real map is drawn.

      Verified = at least 5 more up votes than down votes and 3 out of 4 votes up. Disputed = people disagree. You get one vote per pin and can change it any time. You can delete your own pins from this browser.

      Hive (optional): sign in with Hive Keychain (the browser extension, or the Keychain phone app) and your pins carry your Hive name, your votes count double, and pins that at least 2 Hive accounts vote real get a Hive ✓ badge. Each signed pin or vote is a small public, permanent record on the Hive blockchain (custom_json mes_gtamap, posting key only, no HIVE spent). mes.fm can hide a pin from the map but cannot erase it from the blockchain.
