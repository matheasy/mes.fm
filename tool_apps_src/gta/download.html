Faster download tips

  • Preload from November 10. The download then has nine days instead of launch night, when everyone downloads at once.
  • Plug in a cable if you can: Ethernet is usually much faster and steadier than Wi-Fi.
  • Pause other downloads and streaming on the same internet while it downloads.
  • Keep the console in rest mode with downloads allowed (PS5: Settings › System › Power Saving › Features Available in Rest Mode; Xbox: Sleep mode with updates on).
  • Leave room to spare. Updates and saves need space too, so aim for at least 50 GB free on top.
