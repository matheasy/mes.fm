What to know on launch day

  • It is out now on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (Rockstar, November 19, 2026). There is still no PC version announced.
  • When it unlocked: Rockstar said midnight local time in every region, so New Zealand played first. The PlayStation Store listing put the USA and Canada on one time, midnight Eastern (9 p.m. on the 18th on the West Coast). Xbox Cloud Gaming started at 1 a.m. Eastern.
  • Size: the download needs up to 212 GB depending on the console. Two small updates came out before launch (about 1 GB and 0.6 GB on PS5, about 2.2 GB and 1.6 GB on Xbox). Keep about 50 GB spare for later updates.
  • Official map: Rockstar put a free printable map of Leonida and the cover art poster on its official site; a double-sided paper copy comes to the Rockstar Store shortly after launch.
  • Pre-order bonus: launch day was the last day to get the Vintage Vice City Pack and a free month of GTA+ with the game.
  • Music: Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (34 new songs) came out the same night on the streaming services.
  • Spoilers: Rockstar asked players not to share story spoilers openly, so friends can play Jason and Lucia's story for themselves.
