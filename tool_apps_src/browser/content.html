Recommended browser extensions & apps

Free add-ons that make the web faster to use. The first three are built by MES; the others are made by other developers that we recommend. The extensions work in Chrome, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers: open the link, then press Add to Chrome (Brave and Edge install from the same page).

  • MES Video Speed Controller Made by MESOur own extension: speed any HTML5 video up or down from 0.1x to 16x (YouTube, Rumble, Odysee, 3Speak and most other sites) with keyboard shortcuts and an on-video control. Free, private, no tracking.
  • MES Read Aloud Made by MESOur own text-to-speech extension: listen to any page or selection with the word and sentence being read highlighted on the page. Choose your voice and speed, and keep different setups. Free, private, no tracking.
  • MES Dark Mode Made by MESOur own dark mode (or light mode) for any website: it leaves pages that are already dark alone, keeps photos natural, and lets each site have its own choice, a schedule and saved setups. Free, private, no tracking.
  • Volume BoosterTurn up the audio of a quiet video or tab above the browser's normal maximum.
  • Read Aloud for AndroidThe same text-to-speech reader as an Android app.
  • Archive PageSave a copy of any web page to an archive in one click, so it can still be read if the original disappears.
