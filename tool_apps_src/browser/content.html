MES extensions

Free browser extensions built by MES for Chrome, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers. No accounts, no tracking, readable source.

Dark ModeDark mode (or light mode) for any website, with per-site control, a schedule and saved setups. Read AloudListen to any page or selection with the word and sentence highlighted as they are read. Text SizeA− and A+ for any website: only the text changes, remembered per site. Video Speed ControllerPlay any video from 0.1x to 16x with keyboard shortcuts and an on-video control. Volume BoosterMake any tab louder, up to 600%, with a soft limiter against distortion.
