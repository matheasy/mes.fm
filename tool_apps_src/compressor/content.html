How the compressor works

Pictures
Each picture is redrawn at the size you choose and saved again as JPEG or WebP at the quality you pick, or at the highest quality that fits a size you set (say 200 KB). WebP is usually 25–35% smaller than JPEG at the same look. A PNG kept as PNG can only get smaller by being made smaller in pixels, so for photos choose WebP or JPEG. Pictures with see-through parts keep their transparency when saved as WebP; JPEG gets a white background.
PDFs
Balanced and Strong find the photos and scans inside the PDF and save them again as smaller JPEGs, keeping the text, links and layout untouched, so selectable text stays selectable. Light only rewrites the file's structure. Extreme turns each page into a picture for the smallest file when nothing else is small enough.
Audio
Sound is decoded and saved as MP3 at 64–256 kbps. 128 kbps is a good default for music, 64 kbps mono is plenty for speech. Big WAV and FLAC files shrink by 80–90%.
Video
Video is not supported yet: compressing it in a browser needs a large download and a lot of time, so it will come as its own tool.

Is it private?

Yes. Your files are read and compressed by your own browser. They are never sent to mes.fm or anywhere else, and closing the tab discards everything. Very large files depend on your device's memory.
