    Upscale pictures with AI, free and private

    Make a small or blurry picture 2, 3, 4 or 8 times bigger, or exactly 1080p, 4K or 8K wide. A plain resize just stretches the pixels, so edges get soft and blocky. An AI upscaler has learned what real edges, textures and lines look like, so it fills in the new pixels with believable detail: text and outlines stay sharp, JPEG blocks and noise are cleaned up.

    Which picture type should I choose?

    Photo & everyday
    Photos, screenshots, scans, logos and thumbnails. Uses Real-ESRGAN “general x4v3”, which also removes JPEG blocks and mild noise.
    Drawing, anime & cartoon
    Clean lines and flat colours: anime, cartoons, comics, line art, illustrations and game art. Uses Real-ESRGAN “anime video v3”, which keeps lines crisp without adding photo-like texture.
    Pixel art
    Every pixel becomes a sharp square (nearest neighbour). Use this for retro game sprites and tiny icons, where any smoothing would ruin the look.
    No AI
    A normal high-quality resize, so you can compare and see what the AI adds.

    Questions

    Is it really free with no limits?
    Yes. There is no account, watermark or daily limit, because the work is done by your own device, not a server.
    How long does it take?
    On a computer whose browser can use the graphics chip (WebGPU: Chrome, Edge, Brave and recent Safari), a 1000 × 700 photo takes a few seconds. Without it the AI runs on the processor and the same picture takes a minute or two. The page shows an estimate before you start.
    How big can the result be?
    Up to 16,384 pixels on a side and 80 megapixels, which is a browser limit. For bigger prints upscale in parts in the Photo Editor.
    Can AI recover details that were never in the picture?
    No. It makes believable detail, not the true detail: a face too small to recognise will not become that person, and tiny text may become other letters. For evidence or documents keep the original.
    How does 8× work?
    The models make a picture 4× bigger. For 8× the page upscales 4×, shrinks the result to a quarter of the final size, and upscales 4× again, so the second pass works on the cleaned picture.
    Does it keep transparency?
    Yes, when you save as PNG or WebP. The see-through parts are resized smoothly and the AI works on the colours.
    Which AI models are used?
    Real-ESRGAN by Xintao Wang and others (BSD 3-Clause licence), run by ONNX Runtime Web. The model files are served from mes.fm and kept in your browser after the first use.
