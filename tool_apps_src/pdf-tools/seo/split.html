Split a PDF into separate files or extract pages

Cut a PDF into several smaller PDFs, or pull out just the pages you need. Choose a method after you drop the file:

  • By page range: type ranges like 1-3, 4-6, 7- and each range becomes its own PDF.
  • Every N pages: split into equal chunks, for example every 10 pages (or 1 for one file per page).
  • Extract pages: click the pages you want, or type them (1, 3-5, 9), and save them together as one PDF.

When there is more than one result you can download each file or all of them in a single ZIP.

Questions

How do I save one page of a PDF?
Use Extract pages and click that page (or type its number).
How do I remove pages from a PDF?
Use Organize PDF to delete pages, or extract all the pages you want to keep.
Does splitting reduce quality?
No. Pages are copied unchanged, with their text and images intact.
Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything happens inside your browser, using your own device. Your files never leave it, so it is safe for contracts, IDs and tax papers.
Is there a limit on file size or number of files?
No sign-up and no watermark. Very large files are limited only by your device's memory.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes, in any modern browser, though a computer is more comfortable for many pages.
