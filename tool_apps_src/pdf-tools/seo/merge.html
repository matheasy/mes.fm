Merge PDF files online, free and private

Combine two or more PDFs into a single document. Drop your files in, put them in the order you want by dragging the cards (or with the arrows), and press Merge PDFs. You can also type page numbers under a file, such as 1-3, 5, to use only some of its pages.

How to combine PDFs

  1. Choose or drop your PDF files.
  2. Drag them into the right order. Optionally limit the pages used from each file.
  3. Press Merge PDFs and download the result.

Need to rearrange, rotate or delete individual pages afterwards? Use Organize PDF, or sign and annotate the merged file in the PDF editor.

Questions

Will the merged PDF keep my text and links?
Yes. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and the quality is unchanged. Bookmarks and form fields from the originals may not carry over.
Can I merge password-protected PDFs?
Not directly. Open them in the PDF editor, which asks for the password, and download an unprotected copy first.
Can I merge pictures with PDFs?
Turn the pictures into a PDF with JPG to PDF first, then merge the files.
Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything happens inside your browser, using your own device. Your files never leave it, so it is safe for contracts, IDs and tax papers.
Is there a limit on file size or number of files?
No sign-up and no watermark. Very large files are limited only by your device's memory.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes, in any modern browser, though a computer is more comfortable for many pages.
