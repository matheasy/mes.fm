Rotate, delete and rearrange PDF pages

See every page as a thumbnail and fix the document: drag pages into a new order, rotate sideways scans, delete pages you do not want, duplicate a page, or add a blank one. Press Save the new PDF when it looks right.

How to reorder PDF pages

  1. Drop your PDF to see its pages.
  2. Drag a page to a new place. Use the buttons under a page to rotate it to the left or right, duplicate it, or mark it for deletion (press again to restore it).
  3. Save the new PDF.

To write on, sign or highlight the pages, use the PDF editor. To combine several files first, use Merge PDF.

Questions

Does rotating a page change my original file?
No. It changes the saved copy only.
Can I undo a deleted page?
Yes: a deleted page stays visible (faded) until you save. Press its button again to restore it, or use Reset.
Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything happens inside your browser, using your own device. Your files never leave it, so it is safe for contracts, IDs and tax papers.
Is there a limit on file size or number of files?
No sign-up and no watermark. Very large files are limited only by your device's memory.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes, in any modern browser, though a computer is more comfortable for many pages.
