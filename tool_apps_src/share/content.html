What is the Share Launcher?

Posting one new video or article to 30 social sites usually means opening every site and rewriting the same text again and again. Paste your post once and the Share Launcher writes a version for each site: sized to its character limit, with the right number of hashtags, and with the link where that site wants it. Open copies the text and opens the site's compose or upload page, pre-filled where the site allows it (X, Threads, Bluesky, Gab, Reddit, Truth Social, Pinterest). You still review and press Post yourself.

Where should the link go?

  • In a reply or first comment: X, Facebook and LinkedIn-style feeds show posts with outside links to fewer people. Post the video or text, then reply with the link straight away.
  • Not clickable at all: Instagram and TikTok captions. Say “link in bio”, or use an Instagram Story link sticker.
  • In the post: Threads, Bluesky, Gab, Minds, Truth Social, Discord, Reddit and the Hive / Blurt / Steem sites have no link penalty.
  • In the description: YouTube, Rumble, Odysee, BitChute and 3Speak. On YouTube, also pin a comment with the link.
  • The whole point: Pinterest pins link to your page, so always set the destination link.

Tips

  • Upload the video natively where you can; it reaches far more people than a link to it.
  • Odysee's upload form has its own URL box under Title (odysee.com/@you/<slug>); the Odysee card's Parsed fields include a ready-made slug, editable if you'd rather shorten it.
  • 3Speak, Rumble, Odysee, BitChute, Blurt media and PayChute each get a ready-made Comment block to paste (and pin) after the video is live: "Notes: <your link>" plus any lines you add in Extra reference links, one bullet per line.
  • Use Choose my sites to keep only the sites you use, and ✎ URL on a card to make Open go to your own channel or server.
  • Tick Done as you go: the card folds to one line with a green check. Click a site or section name to fold or unfold it, or use Collapse all for a compact checklist. Ticks are remembered per link, so your next post starts with a fresh list.
  • Give it a few posts, then check your analytics and drop the sites that never send visitors.
