    Take your favourites with you

    Your favourites are stored in this browser only. To use them on another browser or computer, make a short link (it stays up to date as you change things), open it there, and choose Replace mine or Merge. Only your group names and page addresses are saved on our server; no account is needed.

    ☁ Save to Hive

    Save your whole list, or one group, to your own Hive account and load it on any computer or phone with Hive Keychain (the browser extension or the phone app). No mes.fm server: your browser writes it to the blockchain, signed in Keychain.

    What to know before you use it
    • It is permanent. Anything written to Hive stays in the blockchain's history forever. "Hide" only removes a backup from this list.
    • It is public. Anyone can see that your account saved an MES Favourites backup, when, and its size. With Encrypt on they can't read it. With it off, your group names and pages are readable by anyone, forever (fine for a list you want to share).
    • Encryption needs your memo key in Keychain (to save and to open). The Keychain phone app can't use the memo key, so there only public saves are possible.
    • It uses free Resource Credits and your posting key; it can't move any funds.

    How MES Favourites works

    • Add: search for any calculator, tool, tutorial or other mes.fm page and press Add. Choose the group first with “Add to”.
    • Arrange: drag a tile by the dots (or anywhere on it with a mouse) to reorder it or move it to another group. Hold Alt while dropping to copy it instead. With the keyboard, focus the dots and press the arrow keys.
    • Groups: create as many as you like, rename them, drag them by their dots, and fold them with the arrow. A page can sit in several groups: use the copy button on its tile.
    • Sync: make your short link (mes.fm/f/…) and open it on another device to get the same favourites, or save the list (or one group) to your Hive account, encrypted or public, and load it anywhere with Hive Keychain.

    Is my list private?

    Yes. It stays in your browser unless you share your link yourself. A short link stores your group names and page addresses on our server (nothing else, no account); you can delete it any time. Without a short link nothing leaves your browser. A Hive backup goes straight from your browser to your own Hive account; encrypted, only your memo key can open it.
