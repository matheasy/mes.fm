Take your favourites with you

    Your favourites are stored in this browser only. To use them on another browser or computer, copy this link, open it there, and choose Replace mine or Merge. Bookmark the link and it is also a backup. Nothing is uploaded to a server: the list is inside the link itself.

    How MES Favourites works

    • Add: search for any calculator, tool, tutorial or other mes.fm page and press Add. Choose the group first with “Add to”.
    • Arrange: drag a tile by the dots (or anywhere on it with a mouse) to reorder it or move it to another group. Hold Alt while dropping to copy it instead. With the keyboard, focus the dots and press the arrow keys.
    • Groups: create as many as you like, rename them, drag them by their dots, and fold them with the arrow. A page can sit in several groups: use the copy button on its tile.
    • Sync: copy your link to open the same favourites on another device.

    Is my list private?

    Yes. It stays in your browser unless you share your link yourself. The link contains page addresses and group names only.
