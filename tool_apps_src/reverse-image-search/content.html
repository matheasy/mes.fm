Right-click a picture on any site and choose Copy image address (or Copy image link), then paste it here. Links that end in .jpg, .png or .webp work best.

Drop a picture here
or click to choose a file, or press Ctrl/⌘ + V to paste a screenshot or copied image

Your picture stays in this browser until you press Open. Then, unless you turn it off below, a resized copy is kept for one hour at a private link on mes.fm so each site can load it by itself, and is deleted automatically.

Add your own image search site

Paste the site's address with %s (or {u}) where the image link goes, for example https://tineye.com/search?url=%s.

Reverse image search on every engine at once

Reverse image search finds where a picture came from. Instead of visiting Google Lens, Bing, TinEye and Yandex one by one, give the picture once and open all of them together. Paste a link, or upload, drop or paste the picture itself, tick the sites you want and press Open.

What makes this one useful

  • Link or file. Paste an image address, drag a picture in, choose a file, or paste a screenshot straight from your clipboard.
  • Search just part of the picture. Drag a box around the person, object or logo you care about (file mode).
  • Rotate and flip first: many reposts are mirrored, and a flipped copy finds them.
  • Sets for the job: popular engines, finding the original, art and anime sources.
  • Add your own reverse image search site; your choices are remembered on this device.
  • Private: in link mode nothing is uploaded. For a picture file, a resized copy is stored for one hour at a private, unguessable mes.fm link when you press Open, so the sites you tick can load it; it is then deleted. Switch Let the sites load my picture off to keep it entirely on your device.

Questions

Why do some sites need me to press Ctrl+V?

Google Lens, TinEye and some others do not accept a picture sent from another website (they refuse it for security). So with a file, this page copies the picture to your clipboard and opens the site: press Ctrl/⌘ + V there. SauceNAO and IQDB accept the file directly. With a link, every site opens already searching.

How do I find the original source of a photo?

Use the Find the original set. In TinEye, sort the results by Oldest; in Google Lens look for the largest version and the earliest date. Then check who posted it first.

What can I do with a local screenshot if a site only takes links?

Use the paste route (Lens, Bing, Yandex and TinEye all accept a pasted picture), or upload the file to a place you control and paste its link.

Is there face search?

No, on purpose. Face-recognition search sites can be used to track and harass people, so they are not included. You can still add any site yourself with the button above.
