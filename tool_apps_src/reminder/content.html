How to get reminded

  • Calendar (recommended). Add it to your calendar and your phone or computer alerts you at the times you ticked, even when the browser is closed. Weekly events stay at the host’s time across daylight saving changes.
  • This browser. Remind me in this browser saves the event here and shows a notification with a sound, and can read your own message aloud (for example “Reminder: your ICBC driver’s licence test is coming up in 30 minutes”) with a voice you pick. It only works while a tab of this page is open (a pinned tab is handy), so the page asks before you close it; if the tab was closed at the time, you get the alert when you next open it. You can also make a reminder just for yourself: fill in the form below and press Remind me in this browser, no link needed.

Questions

Why not just use YouTube’s “Notify me”?

YouTube’s live notifications are often late, grouped or never sent, depending on your notification settings and how many channels you follow. A calendar alert comes from your own device at the time you chose.

Why does the page ask me to click it, or ask before I close the tab?

Browsers only let a page play a sound or speak after you have clicked or typed on it at least once since it was opened, so a reminder tab that was reopened by itself has to be clicked once. The “Leave site?” question stops you closing the tab by accident while a reminder is still to come (untick Warn me if I close this tab to turn it off). The spoken message uses the voices on your own device; nothing is uploaded.

Can I get an email?

Not yet: an email reminder needs a server that stores your address and sends the message, and this page deliberately stores nothing. Most calendars can send you an email reminder themselves (in Google Calendar, edit the event and add an “Email” notification).

What is stored?

With the full link, nothing on mes.fm: the event is in the link itself. A short link stores the event’s title, time, time zone, link and notes on mes.fm (nothing about you) so the short address can find them; it is kept for a year after the event. The reminders you save with Remind me in this browser stay in this browser only.
