How MES Galleries works

Photos and videos are encrypted in your browser (AES-256, one key per gallery) before they upload to Cloudflare R2, so the storage company only holds scrambled files. A client opens a gallery with its link plus an email address you added, confirmed by a one-time code. Inactive or expired galleries can't be opened. Contracts: pick a template, fill the fields, send; the client signs on the page and you both get an email with a link to the signed PDF (with an audit trail).

Questions

Are e-signatures valid for photography contracts?
Yes in B.C. (Electronic Transactions Act), the rest of Canada (provincial acts on the same model) and the US (federal E-SIGN Act and UETA in 49 states; New York has its own similar law). A photography service agreement is not one of the excluded kinds (wills, land transfers and similar). What makes it stand up is proof of intent and of who signed: the client confirms by the emailed link, ticks consent to sign electronically, types a name and draws a signature, and the PDF records the time, email, browser and a hashed network address plus a fingerprint of the exact text. Have a lawyer look at the contract wording once; that matters more than the signing method.
How big can files be?
Up to 8 GB per file. Videos upload in 8 MB encrypted pieces and play in the page after loading (for very long videos, consider a web-size MP4).
Backups
Keep your originals on your own drive or cloud: this is for delivering, not your only copy. Deleting a gallery deletes its files.
