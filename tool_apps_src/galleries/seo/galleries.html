Private photo and video galleries

Your photographer shared this gallery with you. Open it with the link from their email and the email address they added: we email you a 6-digit code, so nobody else can get in even if the link is passed on. Photos and videos are stored encrypted and are unlocked only in your browser.

Questions

I did not get a code.
Check spam and that you typed the same address your photographer has. Codes last 15 minutes; you can ask for a new one.
How do favourites work?
Tap the heart on any photo or video. Your photographer sees your list. "Favourites" shows only those.
Can I download?
If your photographer allowed it, there is a download button in the viewer and a "Download all" button (web size or full size).
Videos?
Yes: tap a video to load and play it in the page. Long videos take a moment to load first.
Signing an agreement
An agreement link opens the text, a box for your name and a signature box (finger, pen or mouse). After you tick "I agree" and sign, both of you get the signed copy by email and you can download it as a PDF.
