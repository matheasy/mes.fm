Famous birthdays: who shares your birthday?

Type your birthday and see which famous people were born on the same day: actors, musicians, athletes, scientists, writers, presidents and royalty, most famous first. Add the year to find people born on exactly the same date, people born the same year, and the stars whose birthdays are a day or two from yours. Every person shows their Sun sign and Chinese zodiac animal, with a link to compare birth charts.

How it works

  • Who is included. About 10,000 people whose exact day of birth is recorded on Wikidata, the free database behind Wikipedia, ranked by how many Wikipedias and sister projects have a page about them. People with only a birth year are left out.
  • Dates. Dates before the calendar change (1582, later in some countries) are shown in the modern (Gregorian) calendar, as Wikidata stores them, so they can differ from the date printed in old books by 10 to 13 days.
  • Signs. The Sun sign is worked out for that exact date and year (the Sun changes sign on slightly different days each year); on a change day both signs are shown, since the birth time is not known.

Questions

Why is someone famous missing?

Either their exact birth date is not on Wikidata, Wikidata records two different dates (they are left out rather than guessed), or they are just outside the 10,000 most-linked people. Search by name to check.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. The list of people is downloaded once and every search and filter runs in your browser; your birthday is never sent or stored.
