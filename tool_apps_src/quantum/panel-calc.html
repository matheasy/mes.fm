Quick quantum numbers, live as you type. Type a value with its unit (500 nm, 2 eV, 1e-10 m, 0.5c, 25 °C) or pick the unit from the menu. Each card shows the formula, your numbers plugged in, and what the answer means.

de Broglie wavelength

\( \displaystyle \lambda = \frac{h}{p}, \qquad p = \frac{\sqrt{K^2 + 2Kmc^2}}{c} \;\approx\; \sqrt{2mK}, \qquad K = qV \)

Photon energy, wavelength and frequency

\( \displaystyle E = h f = \frac{hc}{\lambda}, \qquad E\,[\text{eV}] \approx \frac{1239.84}{\lambda\,[\text{nm}]}, \qquad p = \frac{E}{c} \)

Heisenberg uncertainty

\( \displaystyle \Delta x\,\Delta p \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}, \qquad \Delta v \ge \frac{\hbar}{2m\,\Delta x}, \qquad \Delta E\,\Delta t \gtrsim \frac{\hbar}{2} \)

Tunnelling through a rectangular barrier

\( \displaystyle T = \left[1 + \frac{V^2\sinh^2(\kappa a)}{4E(V-E)}\right]^{-1}, \quad \kappa = \frac{\sqrt{2m(V-E)}}{\hbar}, \qquad T \approx \frac{16E(V-E)}{V^2}\,e^{-2\kappa a} \)

Particle in a box (and dye colours)

\( \displaystyle E_n = \frac{n^2 h^2}{8 m L^2}, \qquad \Delta E_{\text{HOMO}\to\text{LUMO}} = \frac{h^2 (N+1)}{8 m_e L^2}, \qquad \lambda = \frac{hc}{\Delta E} \)

Bohr model (hydrogen-like ions)

\( \displaystyle r_n = \frac{n^2 a_0}{Z}\frac{m_e}{\mu}, \qquad v_n = \frac{Z\alpha c}{n}, \qquad E_n = -\frac{13.6\ \text{eV}\,Z^2}{n^2}\frac{\mu}{m_e}, \qquad L = n\hbar \)

Rydberg formula and spectral line finder

\( \displaystyle \frac{1}{\lambda} = R_\infty \frac{\mu}{m_e} Z^2 \left( \frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2} \right), \qquad R_\infty = 10\,973\,731.568\ \text{m}^{-1} \)

Compton scattering

\( \displaystyle \lambda' - \lambda = \frac{h}{mc}\,(1 - \cos\theta), \qquad \frac{h}{m_e c} = 2.426\ \text{pm} \)

Blackbody radiation

\( \displaystyle \lambda_{\text{peak}} = \frac{b}{T}, \quad b = 2.898\times10^{-3}\ \text{m K}, \qquad j = \sigma T^4, \quad B_\lambda = \frac{2hc^2}{\lambda^5}\frac{1}{e^{hc/\lambda k_B T} - 1} \)

Thermal de Broglie wavelength

\( \displaystyle \lambda_{\text{th}} = \frac{h}{\sqrt{2\pi m k_B T}}, \qquad n\lambda_{\text{th}}^3 \ll 1 \text{ classical gas}, \quad n\lambda_{\text{th}}^3 \gtrsim 1 \text{ quantum gas} \)

Constants: CODATA 2018 (h, e and c are exact in SI). Non-relativistic formulas are used where the card says so; the de Broglie card is fully relativistic. The tunnelling and box cards are idealized models (flat barrier, infinitely high walls).
