Conception date calculator

Work backwards from your due date, the first day of your last period or the age of the pregnancy to find the most likely conception date, the days when sex could have led to the pregnancy, and your estimated ovulation date. It also shows the equivalent last-period date doctors use to count your weeks.

How it works

  • Conception is about 266 days (38 weeks) before the due date, which is about two weeks after the first day of your last period in a 28-day cycle.
  • Sperm can live up to 5 days and the egg about a day, so sex in the five days before ovulation and on ovulation day can all lead to pregnancy. The calculator shows that whole window.
  • If your cycle is longer or shorter than 28 days, enter it and the date moves with it.

Questions

How accurate is an estimated conception date?

It is an estimate of a few days either way. A due date from an early ultrasound is the most accurate base. Dates from the last period assume you ovulated on a typical day for your cycle length.

Why does my doctor say I am two weeks further along than my conception date suggests?

Pregnancy weeks are counted from the first day of the last period, about two weeks before conception. A baby conceived on a given date is “2 weeks pregnant” on that day.
