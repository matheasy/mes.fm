Pregnancy due date calculator

Work out your due date from the first day of your last period, the date of conception, an IVF embryo transfer (3-, 5- or 6-day embryos) or an ultrasound scan. You get today's gestational age in weeks and days, your trimester, days to go, a progress bar, key dates (heartbeat, anatomy scan, third trimester, full term) and a week-by-week calendar you can add to your phone.

How the due date is calculated

  • Last period: add 280 days (40 weeks), adjusted if your cycle is longer or shorter than 28 days (Naegele's rule).
  • Conception date: add 266 days (38 weeks).
  • IVF transfer: add 261 days for a day-5 embryo, 263 for day 3, 260 for day 6.
  • Ultrasound: 280 days minus the gestational age the scan reports on the scan date.

Questions

How accurate is a due date?

Only about 4 in 100 babies are born on their due date. Most arrive between 37 and 42 weeks. Your due date is the middle of that range, not a deadline. An early ultrasound (before about 14 weeks) is the most accurate way to date a pregnancy, and your midwife or doctor may move the date if the scan disagrees with your period dates by more than about a week.

Why is pregnancy counted from my last period and not from conception?

Because the date of the last period is usually known and conception is not. That is why you are already “2 weeks pregnant” on the day you ovulate.

When does each trimester start?

The first trimester runs to the end of week 13, the second trimester is weeks 14–27 and the third starts at week 28. The calculator shows the exact dates for your pregnancy.

What if I do not know when my last period was?

Choose “An ultrasound scan” and enter the scan date and the gestational age on the report. If you do not have a scan yet, ask your doctor or midwife for a dating scan.
