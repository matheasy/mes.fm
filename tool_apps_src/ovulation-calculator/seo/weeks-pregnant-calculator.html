How many weeks pregnant am I?

Enter the first day of your last period (or your conception date, an IVF transfer, a scan, or your due date) and see exactly how many weeks and days pregnant you are today, your trimester, how long is left, and a week-by-week list with the start date of every week and the size your baby is compared with a fruit or vegetable.

Good to know

  • Weeks are counted from the first day of your last period, not from conception. A full pregnancy is 40 weeks.
  • Trimesters: first to the end of week 13, second weeks 14–27, third from week 28.
  • Term: 37 to 38+6 weeks is early term, 39 to 40+6 full term, 41 to 41+6 late term, and 42 weeks or more post-term.
  • The size comparisons and measurements are typical averages, and healthy babies vary.

Questions

How many months is 20 weeks?

Pregnancy is about 9 months, and a month is 4.3 weeks, so 20 weeks is a little under 5 months. Doctors prefer weeks because months are different lengths.

Can I be 6 weeks pregnant when I only conceived 4 weeks ago?

Yes. Because weeks are counted from your last period, which comes about two weeks before conception, you are always about two weeks “ahead” of the real age of the embryo.
