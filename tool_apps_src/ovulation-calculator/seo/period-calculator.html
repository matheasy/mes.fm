Period calculator and cycle tracker

Find out when your next period is due, see the following twelve months at a glance and keep a simple log of your cycles. Enter the first day of your last period; add earlier periods to the log and the calculator replaces the textbook 28 days with your own average, shows how regular you are and gives a range of dates when your cycles vary.

Questions

How is my next period predicted?

It is the first day of your last period plus your cycle length. If you log two or more periods, your average of the last six cycles is used.

What is a normal cycle length?

For adults, 21 to 35 days is considered normal, and the length can vary by a few days from month to month. Teenagers' cycles can be longer, and cycles often become shorter and less regular in the years before menopause.

Why is my period late?

Pregnancy is one reason, but stress, illness, travel, big changes in weight or exercise, breastfeeding, thyroid problems and conditions such as PCOS can all delay a period. If you could be pregnant, take a test from the first day of the missed period. See a doctor if you miss three periods in a row or your cycles suddenly change.

Is it a problem if my cycle is irregular?

Cycles that differ by more than 7–9 days from month to month, or that are shorter than 21 or longer than 38 days, are worth discussing with a doctor. A log like this one gives them useful information.
