    A free portfolio tracker for stocks, funds and crypto

    Add your trades once and see what everything is worth now, what it cost you, your gain or loss on each holding, dividends and interest, and your realized gains for each tax year. Stocks and ETFs from most exchanges (US, Canada, UK, Europe, Asia), mutual funds, and thousands of cryptocurrencies are priced automatically; anything else (a house, gold coins, a private company) can be added with your own price.

    How is my gain worked out?

    Each buy adds to your cost (fees included). When you sell, the cost of the units sold is taken away using the method you pick: average cost (the adjusted cost base used in Canada: every unit costs the average of what you paid) or first in, first out (the oldest units are sold first). Realized gain = what you got for the sale minus fees minus that cost. Unrealized gain = today's value of what you still hold minus its cost.

    Different currencies

    Every holding keeps its own currency (a Toronto stock in CAD, a US stock in USD, crypto in USD) and totals are shown in the currency you choose. For tax records you can type the exchange rate of the trade's day into "Rate that day"; otherwise today's rate is used for the cost too.

    Wallets

    Paste a public Bitcoin, Ethereum, Hive, XRP or Solana address and its balance is read live from the blockchain and added to your holdings. A public address can only be read, never spent, so it is safe to use. Hive balances include Hive Power, savings and HBD. Ethereum wallets also count their ERC-20 tokens: about 50 popular ones (stablecoins, wrapped bitcoin, staked ether, LINK, UNI and so on) always, and any other token only when it really trades (over $250,000 a day and worth over $10 million in total), because wallets get sent lots of worthless "tokens" that claim big prices. You can hide any token on a wallet with its ✕. The same 0x address is also read on Base, Arbitrum and BNB Chain: its ETH or BNB plus a built-in list of their popular tokens (USDC, USDT, WETH, cbBTC, AERO, ARB, GMX, BTCB, CAKE and more). Polygon, Optimism, Avalanche and Solana tokens are not read yet.

    Importing from a broker or exchange

    Most brokers and exchanges let you download your transaction history as a CSV file. Drop it on the Import tab: the date, type, symbol, quantity, price and fee columns are found by their names (you can change them), staking and reward income becomes coins received at that day's value, and rows that can't be read are listed so nothing disappears silently.

    Is my data private?

    Yes. There is no account and your portfolio is never uploaded: it is kept in this browser. To look prices up the page sends only the list of symbols (never quantities or amounts) and, for wallets, the public address. You can keep a backup file, save it to your own cloud folder, or save it encrypted to the Hive blockchain to open it on another device.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. It is a record-keeping tool. Prices can be delayed or missing, and tax rules differ by country: check with an accountant before filing.
