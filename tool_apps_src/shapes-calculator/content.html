Area, surface area and volume: what is the difference?

Area measures flat space (square units: m², ft²). Surface area is the total area of the outside of a 3D solid: add up every face. Volume is the space inside a solid (cubic units: m³, in³), which is also its capacity: 1,000 cm³ = 1 litre. Perimeter and circumference are distances around a flat shape.

Questions

How do I find the volume of a cylinder?
V = πr²h. A can with radius 3 cm and height 10 cm holds π × 9 × 10 ≈ 282.7 cm³, about 0.28 litres.
Why does doubling a length multiply the area by 4 and the volume by 8?
Area depends on two lengths multiplied together and volume on three, so scaling every length by k scales areas by k² and volumes by k³. The “work it backwards” box uses this.
Are the ellipse perimeter and ellipsoid surface area exact?
No exact formula exists for either. This page uses Ramanujan's ellipse approximation (accurate to a tiny fraction of a percent for all but extreme shapes) and Knud Thomsen's ellipsoid formula (within about 1%).
