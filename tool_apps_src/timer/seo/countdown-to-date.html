Countdown to a date

See exactly how many days, hours, minutes and seconds are left until Christmas, a birthday, a wedding, an exam, a holiday, a launch or any date you choose, ticking live. Add as many events as you like (up to 12): they are saved in your browser.

How to use it

  • Type a name, pick a date (and a time if it matters) and press + Add, or use a Quick add button for Christmas, New Year and more.
  • Press the expand button on an event for a full-screen countdown.
  • When the moment arrives the event says so, and shows how long ago it was.
  • Share an event with a link like mes.fm/countdown-to-date?date=2026-12-25&name=Christmas.

Questions

Which time zone does it use?

Your device’s local time zone. A date with no time counts down to midnight at the start of that day. To plan across time zones, use the Time Zone Converter; to count days between two dates, use the Days Between Dates Calculator.
