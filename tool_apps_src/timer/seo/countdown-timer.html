Online countdown timer

A simple, accurate countdown timer for anything you need to time: cooking, tea, exams, presentations, workouts, laundry or a quick break. Type a time such as 25, 1h 30m or 2:30, or press a preset from 30 seconds to an hour, then press Start.

Features

  • Run several timers at once, each with its own label. Drag the ⠿ handle to put them in your order.
  • +1 minute while it runs, and pause, resume or restart any time.
  • Full screen for a big countdown that is readable from across the room.
  • The remaining time in the browser tab, so you can keep working in another window.
  • A running timer survives a reload, and a sound, volume and repeat setting you choose: once, three times or until you stop it.
  • A link such as mes.fm/countdown-timer?t=10m&label=Tea&start=1 starts a labelled timer straight away.

Questions

How do I set a timer for 90 minutes?

Type 90 (a bare number is minutes) or 1h 30m into the box, or use the h / m / s boxes, then press Start.

Does it keep running if I close the tab?

It keeps its time (the end moment is saved), but a browser cannot ring while the page is closed. Reopen the page and it tells you the timer finished. Keep the tab open for the alarm to sound.
