Online alarm clock

Set one or more alarms for a time of day and let your browser ring them: wake-up reminders, meetings, medication, a break. Pick the time, add a label, tick Daily to repeat it every day, and turn it on. When it rings you can stop it or snooze for 5 or 10 minutes.

Good to know

  • Keep the page open. A website cannot ring after you close it, so leave the tab open (it can be in the background) and your computer awake and unmuted. For overnight use, a phone’s built-in alarm is the safer choice.
  • Up to six alarms at once, each with its own label and daily switch.
  • Alarms use your device’s local time and daylight saving time automatically.
  • If an alarm went off while the page was closed, you are told when you come back.
  • Choose a friendly or annoying alarm, beeps or a chime, and the volume, in Sound and options. Press Test sound once so your browser allows audio.

Questions

Can I set an alarm for a few minutes from now?

Yes, but a countdown timer is simpler for that: type 10 and press Start.
