    About this calculator

    This free online calculator covers everyday arithmetic and the maths you need for school: addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, squares and square roots, cubes and cube roots, powers, percentages, factorials, logarithms and trigonometry in degrees or radians. Type a whole expression such as (12 + 8) × 3² and the answer appears as you type, following the correct order of operations.

    • Exact big-number multiplication and division with quotient, remainder and repeating decimals.
    • Squares, cubes and roots with simplified radicals (√72 = 6√2) and a perfect-square check.
    • Fractions added, subtracted, multiplied and divided and always reduced, plus decimal-to-fraction conversion.
    • Prime factorization, factors, GCD and LCM, number base conversion and Roman numerals, and mean, median, mode and standard deviation of any list.
    • Your history stays in your own browser, and every calculation has a shareable link.
