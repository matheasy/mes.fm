Learn angles and geometry by moving things

This lab lets you drag points and lines and watch the angles recalculate. In the Triangles tab, move the corners to see the angle sum stay at 180°, how the triangle is named (acute, right or obtuse; scalene, isosceles or equilateral), its area, circumcircle, incircle, centroid and orthocentre, and add a similar triangle to see why corresponding angles stay equal while sides and areas scale. Questions

How do I know if two triangles are similar?
If two angles of one equal two angles of the other (AA), or all three side ratios match (SSS), or two side ratios and the angle between them match (SAS).
What is a transversal?
A line that crosses two other lines. It makes eight angles; when the two lines are parallel the angles fall into just two sizes, θ and 180° − θ.
Is my data private?
Nothing is sent anywhere: it all runs in your browser.
