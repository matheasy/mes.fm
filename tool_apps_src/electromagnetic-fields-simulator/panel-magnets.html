Bar magnets and the two families of lines around them. Flux lines (the usual “magnetic field lines”) show which way B points; they leave the N end, curve round and go back in at the S end, and carry on inside the magnet from S to N, so every line is a closed loop. Isopotentials are lines of equal magnetic scalar potential ψ, like the contour lines on a map; outside the magnet they cross the flux lines at right angles. Drag a magnet to move it, grab its round handle (or use the mouse wheel over it) to turn it.
