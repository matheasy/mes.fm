Where the numbers come from

  • iPhone & iPad: Apple's daily Sales and Trends reports (App Store Connect). A day's report arrives the next morning (Pacific time), so the newest day is yesterday. Apple counts first-time downloads, re-downloads (the same Apple account installing again, e.g. on a new phone) and updates. Apple does not report uninstalls or how many phones still have the app.
  • Android: Google Play's installs reports. Google writes them once a day and they run 1-3 days behind. Uninstalls and active devices (phones with the app installed now) come only from Google.
  • Ratings: the App Store's public rating (US store) and Google Play's average rating.
  • Numbers are refreshed every few hours. Nothing about you is collected by this page; the counts are the stores' own totals.
