Make any tab louder, up to 600%

A free browser extension by MES for Chrome, Brave and Edge. Quiet video, a soft-spoken lecture, a podcast recorded too low: open the popup, drag the slider (or press Alt+Shift+Up) and that tab gets louder than the browser's normal maximum. It works on any sound the tab plays, including video and audio from other sites, and a soft limiter keeps the loud peaks from clipping.

Private by design: no accounts, no tracking, no network requests. The sound never leaves your computer. The code is plain JavaScript you can read.

Install it (about a minute)

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. You get a folder called mes-volume-booster. Keep it somewhere permanent (Documents is fine): the browser reads the extension from that folder.
  2. Open the extensions page: chrome://extensions in Chrome, brave://extensions in Brave, edge://extensions in Edge.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (a switch at the top right, or on the left in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped mes-volume-booster folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin MES Volume Booster.

Chrome may show a "disable developer mode extensions" notice when it starts: choose Cancel / Keep. Brave and Edge usually do not. Until the extension is listed in the Chrome Web Store, updates are manual: download the new zip, replace the folder and press the reload arrow on the extensions page. Firefox is not supported yet.

How to use it

  • Open the page with the quiet sound, click the toolbar icon and drag the slider or tap a preset (150%, 200%, 300%, 400%, 600%). The icon shows the level as a badge.
  • Alt+Shift+Up / Down make the current tab louder / quieter without opening the popup. Change the shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • Turn off the boost on this tab in the popup to go back to normal and release the tab.
  • Settings: the highest boost (200%, 400% or 600%), the shortcut step, the soft limiter, and whether it remembers each site's last level.

Good to know

  • Protect your ears and speakers. A boost of 300% is about +9.5 dB and 600% about +15.6 dB. Start low and go up slowly, especially with headphones. The popup shows the boost in decibels.
  • Why the browser shows a recording dot. To make a tab louder the extension receives that tab's sound from the browser (the browser's tab-capture feature), changes the volume and plays it back. Chrome marks such a tab with a small indicator. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • The soft limiter rounds off the loud peaks that a big boost creates instead of letting them clip. Quiet sound is boosted by exactly the percentage shown. Turn it off for the raw boost.
  • Limits. It cannot boost browser pages (chrome://), the Web Store, or a tab that another extension is already capturing. Chrome 116 or newer is needed. Boosting is per tab: other tabs stay as they are.

Permissions

tabCapture gets the sound of the tab you boost (only after you click the icon or press the shortcut); offscreen lets a hidden page do the audio processing; activeTab lets the popup know which tab and site you are on; storage keeps your settings and remembered levels. It does not ask to read any website's content.

Questions

Why is there no sound after I turn the boost on?
Another extension or tool may be capturing the same tab. Close it, reload the tab and try again. If the page's audio comes from another tab or a different app, boost that one instead.
Why does a very loud boost sound harsh?
Boosting amplifies noise and any distortion that is already in the recording, and the limiter can only smooth the peaks. Use the lowest level that is comfortable.
Will it make my whole computer louder?
No, only the tab you pick. Your system volume is not changed.
Is it open source?
The whole extension is plain, readable JavaScript served from this site: background.js, offscreen.js, shared.js, manifest.json.
