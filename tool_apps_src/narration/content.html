Your narration

    How the narration tool works

    Type or paste a script and press Make the narration. The text is cut into sentences, each one is spoken by a voice model running in your browser, and the pieces are joined with the pauses you choose. Listen to each line on its own, make any line again, then download the whole thing as MP3, M4A or WAV, or drop it straight into the MES Video Editor.

    Ready-made voices

    28 English voices (American and British, men and women) from the open Kokoro-82M model. The first time, the model (about 90 MB) is downloaded and kept by the browser; it then works offline on any computer, and on most phones too (more slowly).

    Your own voice

    Record or upload 10 to 20 seconds of your speech once, save it, and every script after that can be read in a voice that sounds like you. This uses the open Chatterbox model by Resemble AI (MIT licence). It is big (about 1.5 GB, downloaded once) and needs a computer with a graphics chip the browser can use (WebGPU: Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera on Windows, Mac or ChromeOS). Without one it still works, but very slowly.

    Questions

    Is my voice uploaded anywhere?
    No. The recording is kept in this browser (IndexedDB) and the voice model runs here. Delete a voice with its × button.
    How do I get a better copy of my voice?
    Use a clean, close recording with no echo or music, 10 to 20 seconds long, spoken in the style you want. Try a few samples and keep the best one.
    Can I use someone else's voice?
    Only with their permission. Copying a person's voice without consent to mislead people is wrong and in many places illegal.
    Which languages work?
    English. Other languages are read with English pronunciation.
    How do I add pauses?
    A blank line starts a new paragraph (a longer pause). Type [pause] for 1 second or [pause 2.5] for 2.5 seconds anywhere in the text.
