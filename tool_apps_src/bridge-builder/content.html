How to build
  • Build (✏️): press on a joint or anywhere and drag; let go to make a member. Joints snap to a 0.5 m grid (hold Shift to place freely). The dashed circle shows the longest piece of that material.
  • The road must reach from one bank to the other: use a road material (dark steel or timber deck) for it. Cars only drive on road members.
  • Joints are pins, like a real truss: a member can only pull or push. A road on its own folds up, so brace it with triangles.
  • Move (✋) drags joints, Select (👆) shows a member's force and lets you change it, Delete removes. Two fingers, the right mouse button or Space + drag pans; Ctrl + wheel or pinch zooms.
  • Keys: B M S D tools, 1-6 materials, [ ] size, Ctrl+Z undo, Space runs the test, F fits the view.

How it vibrates

Every structure has natural frequencies. Shaking at one of them (wind, marching, an earthquake) makes the movement grow: that is resonance. Tap a mode to watch it.
