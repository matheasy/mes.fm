⬢ Play on Hive

Every shot is signed with your Hive account (the Hive Keychain extension or the Keychain phone app) and kept on the Hive blockchain for good. No mes.fm server decides anything: both browsers read the shots from the chain and run them through the same physics, which is written so every browser gets exactly the same result, so the whole game and its result are public and can't be changed. A shot takes about 3 to 6 seconds to reach the other player and costs no HIVE (only a little of your account's free Resource Credits).
