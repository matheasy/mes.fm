9-ball pool, free and with real physics

9-ball is the fast rotation game of professional pool. Nine balls are racked in a diamond with the 1 at the front and the 9 in the middle. You must always hit the lowest numbered ball first, but any ball that goes in keeps you at the table, and the 9 going in on a legal shot wins straight away, even in a combination or on the break. 10-ball (ten balls in a triangle, pot the 10 to win) is in the game list too. Play the computer at five levels (up to Expert), a friend on one device, or someone online; the MES Billiards page has 8-ball, snooker and practice challenges.

The rules used here

Lowest ball first
The cue ball must touch the lowest numbered ball on the table before any other ball.
Cushion rule
After the hit, a ball must go in or some ball must touch a cushion (except on the break).
Fouls
The cue ball going in, missing, hitting the wrong ball first or no cushion: the other player gets ball in hand anywhere. A 9 potted on a foul goes back on its spot.
Not used
The push-out after the break and the three-fouls-in-a-row loss are not part of this version.

9-ball tips

Look for the 9 combination
When the lowest ball sits near the 9 and the 9 is near a pocket, playing the lowest ball into the 9 is often the best shot on the table.
Position, position
You can't choose your next ball, so every shot is about where the cue ball stops for the next number. Use the spin control: follow to go forward, draw to come back, side spin to change the angle off a cushion.
Ball in hand is huge
After a foul you may put the cue ball anywhere: give yourself a straight-in shot with an easy angle to the next ball.
