You pay per message, in HBD

No subscription, no account, no card. Each message shows its price before you send it. You pay that price in HBD (Hive's dollar coin) to @mes with Hive Keychain, the answer comes straight back, and that is the whole deal.

How the price is worked out
  • Price = what the AI company charges us for that message × 1.5, rounded up to 0.001 HBD (smallest price 0.001 HBD). The extra half keeps mes.fm running.
  • What they charge depends on the model, on how much text is sent (your message plus the whole chat before it, which the AI rereads every time) and on the answer length you pick.
  • The answer can never cost more than you paid: it is cut off at the length you chose. A short answer costs the same as a long one at the same setting: nothing is kept on account, so there is no change to give back. Pick Short for quick questions.
  • Long chats get dearer with every message. Start a new chat when the topic changes.
  • Payments are final. If the AI fails after you paid, you get a free Try again for that message (within 24 hours).

Privacy: your messages go from our server to Anthropic (chat) or OpenAI (pictures) to make the answer; mes.fm keeps the request for at most an hour (to match it to your payment) and never stores the answer. Your chats and pictures are kept only in this browser. Details in our privacy policy.

About MES AI

MES AI lets you use top AI models without a monthly plan: Anthropic's Claude for chat (Sonnet 5.5 for everyday work, Opus 5.5 for hard coding and research) and OpenAI's GPT Image 2 for pictures. You pay a few cents' worth of HBD per message on the Hive blockchain, and only for what you use.

Questions

Do I need an account?
Only a Hive account, with the Hive Keychain browser extension or the Keychain phone app. No email, no password on mes.fm. No Hive account yet? Get one at signup.hive.io.
Why pay per message instead of a subscription?
You only pay for what you use, nothing renews, and there is no balance that could be lost. The price is shown before every message.
Why did my answer stop in the middle?
It reached the answer length you paid for. Send "continue" (a new message) or pick Medium or Long next time. Opus thinks before it writes, and that thinking counts toward the length too.
Something went wrong after I paid.
Press Try again on that message: one free retry is kept for 24 hours. If that fails too, contact us with the memo of your payment (it starts with mesai:).
Where is my chat history?
Only in this browser. Clearing the browser's site data removes it. Use Download to keep a chat as a Markdown file.
