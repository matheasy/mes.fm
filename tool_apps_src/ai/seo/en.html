About MES AI

MES AI lets you use top AI models without a monthly plan: Anthropic's Claude (Sonnet 5.5 for everyday work, Opus 5.5 for hard coding and research) or OpenAI's ChatGPT models (GPT-6.2 Sol and Astra) for chat, OpenAI's GPT Image 2 or xAI's Grok Imagine for pictures, and Grok Imagine for short videos with sound. You pay a few cents' worth of HBD per message on the Hive blockchain, and only for what you use.

Questions

Is there a free model that needs no Hive account?
Yes: the three models under Free, on this device (Llama 3.2 1B, Qwen3 1.7B and Llama 3.2 3B). They run inside your browser on your own graphics chip with WebLLM, so they cost nothing, need no login, and nothing you type is sent to mes.fm or to any AI company. The first time, the model is downloaded once (about 0.7 to 1.8 GB) and your browser keeps it; Remove downloaded model frees the space. They need WebGPU (Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer, or Safari 26 and newer) and enough memory, read text only, remember only a short chat, and are much weaker than the paid models: fine for quick questions, drafts and simple code. If your device can’t run them, use the free daily messages instead.
Do I need an account?
Only a Hive account, with the Hive Keychain browser extension or the Keychain phone app. No email, no password on mes.fm. No Hive account yet? Get one at signup.hive.io.
Why pay per message instead of a subscription?
You only pay for what you use, nothing renews, and there is no balance that could be lost. The price is shown before every message.
Why did my answer stop in the middle?
It reached the answer length you paid for. Send "continue" (a new message) or pick Medium or Long next time. Opus and the ChatGPT models think before they write, and that thinking counts toward the length too.
Can I make videos with OpenAI's Sora?
No: OpenAI shut down Sora 2 and its video service for developers on September 24, 2026. Videos here are made by xAI's Grok Imagine.
Something went wrong after I paid.
Press Try again on that message: one free retry is kept for 24 hours. If that fails too, contact us with the memo of your payment (it starts with mesai:).
Where is my chat history?
Only in this browser. Clearing the browser's site data removes it. Use Download to keep a chat as a Markdown file.
