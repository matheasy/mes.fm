Grade 7 numeracy practice

Questions cover Grade 7 math in B.C.: integers on a number line and with all four operations, order of operations, decimals in money and measurement, percent of a number, discounts and B.C. sales tax (GST + PST), percent increase, adding and subtracting fractions, changing between fractions, decimals and percents, ratios and recipes, unit prices and speed, one- and two-step equations, linear patterns and expressions, area of triangles, parallelograms and trapezoids, circles with π, angles, coordinates and translations, mean, median, mode and range, and probability with spinners and dice.

Questions

How do I type a negative number?
Use the minus key, for example -7. Fractions can be typed as 3/4 and mixed numbers as 1 1/2 where a number is asked for.
Which value of π should I use?
The questions say π ≈ 3.14, but answers worked out with your calculator's π button are also marked right.
