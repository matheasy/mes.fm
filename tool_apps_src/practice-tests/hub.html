How these practice tests work

  • New questions every time. Numeracy questions are made from templates with random numbers, so no two tests are the same. Literacy tests pick passages at random from a bank of original stories, articles, poems and notices.
  • Three ways to practise: a full test (25 numeracy questions or 4 reading passages), a quick 10, or practise a topic with the answer shown after each question.
  • Every question shows how hard it is (easy, medium, hard) and which grade and age it fits.
  • Results by topic, then review every question with a worked explanation. Print a blank test with an answer key, or save your results as a PDF.
  • Private: no account, nothing is uploaded. Unfinished tests and your results stay in this browser.
