Blue Widget

The Blue Widget is our most popular widget. It is made from one piece of aircraft-grade aluminium, so it is strong enough for the workshop and light enough to carry in a pocket. Each widget is anodised in a deep blue that does not chip or fade, and the edges are rounded by hand so it is comfortable to hold for a long time.

What it does

Use it to open paint tins, scrape glue, tighten small screws, measure up to ten centimetres and hang tools on the wall. The hole at one end fits a key ring, and the notch at the other end lifts staples without tearing paper. People also use it as a bottle opener, a ruler for sketches and a wedge for doors that will not stay open.

Sizes and care

Sizes

It comes in two sizes: the pocket size is 10 cm long and weighs 18 grams, the workshop size is 16 cm and weighs 31 grams. Both have the same shape and the same tools.

Care

Wipe it with a damp cloth. It is safe in the dishwasher, but hand washing keeps the colour bright for longer. Do not use it as a pry bar on anything heavy, because aluminium bends before it breaks.

Shipping and returns

Shipping is free anywhere in Canada and takes two to five working days. If it is not what you expected, send it back within thirty days for a full refund. Every widget has a two-year warranty against defects.
