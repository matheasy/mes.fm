How to play Spider

Spider uses two decks (104 cards) dealt into ten columns: the first four columns get six cards, the other six get five, and only the bottom card of each column is face up. The other 50 cards wait in the stock at the top left.

  • Moving cards: any card can go on a card one rank higher, of any suit (a 7 on an 8). A group of cards can only move together when they are all the same suit and in order (for example 9♠ 8♠ 7♠).
  • Turning cards: when you move the last face-up card off a column, the card under it turns over.
  • Empty columns: any card or same-suit run can go into an empty column. Empty columns are the most useful thing in the game: keep them for when you need to move a long run.
  • Finishing a run: build a full run from King down to Ace in one suit and it leaves the table by itself, to the spots at the top right. Clear all eight runs to win.
  • Dealing: when you are stuck, click the stock to deal one new card onto every column. Every column needs at least one card before you can deal.

One, two or four suits

With one suit every card is a spade, so every run you build can move as a group and most games can be won: a good place to start. Two suits (spades and hearts) means you have to keep the colours apart if you want to move runs. Four suits is the classic hard game that even good players win only some of the time.

Tips for winning more Spider games

  • Turn face-down cards over first: each one you turn gives you more choices.
  • When you can put a card on one of two higher cards, choose the one of the same suit.
  • Empty a column before you deal, and do not fill an empty column with a single low card unless it frees something.
  • Before you deal, make every move that tidies a column, because the new row covers every column.
  • Stuck? Press Hint, or Undo a few moves and try another way: undo never counts against you.

Score, daily deal and leaderboard

You start with 500 points, lose one point per move and get 100 for each finished run. Every UTC day there is one Spider deal (one suit) that is the same for everyone in the world. After a win you can save it to the Hive blockchain with "Save to Hive" (one small public message signed with your posting key through Hive Keychain): mes.fm replays your moves on the same deal before the win goes on the leaderboard.

Questions

Is this Spider Solitaire free?
Yes, free with no account. It also keeps working offline after your first visit.
Is every Spider game winnable?
Almost every one-suit game can be won with careful play. Some four-suit deals cannot be won at all, so do not feel bad about losing one.
Is my game kept if I close the page?
Yes: the game in progress is kept in this browser and comes back when you open the page again.
Can I replay the same deal?
Yes: Restart plays the same cards again, and the Link button gives you a link to that deal to send to a friend.
