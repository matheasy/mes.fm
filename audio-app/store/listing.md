## Description (both stores)

MES Audio records your voice and cleans it up with AI, right on your phone.

Tap Record, talk, and tap Stop. Recording keeps going with the screen off or while you use another app, so long talks, lectures and interviews are safe.

Then make it sound better:
• Remove noise: an AI made for speech (DeepFilterNet 3) takes out fans, air conditioning, traffic, hiss and room noise while keeping the voice natural. It runs on the phone, even without a connection.
• Enhance speech: one tap for noise removal, a low cut, voice clarity, a gentle compressor and podcast loudness.
• Compare: switch between Original, Cleaned and Noise only while it plays, and Undo any step.
• Trim: drag across the waveform, then keep that part or delete it.
• Save or share as M4A, MP3 or WAV: to Files / Downloads, Messages, email, Drive or any app.

Need more? The full editor is one tap away: several tracks, fades, EQ, compressor, limiter, hum removal, de-click, loudness to -14 or -16 LUFS, markers and a spectrogram. It can also open the sound of a video.

Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Nothing you record is uploaded.

Made by Math Easy Solutions (mes.fm). The same editor runs in any browser at mes.fm/audio.
