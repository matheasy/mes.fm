## Description

Make quiet videos louder. MES Volume Booster raises the volume of videos and audio (YouTube and more) beyond the site's own maximum, up to 600%.

What it does
• Video mode (default): boosts the page's own video and audio, with no recording indicator.
• Whole-tab mode: boosts everything the tab plays (uses the browser's tab audio capture).
• Soft limiter rounds off peaks so loud parts don't crackle.
• Remembers the level per site; badge shows the boost; shortcuts Alt+Shift+Up / Down.

Free, no ads, no account and no tracking. Sound is processed on your device and never recorded or sent anywhere. Made by MES (mes.fm). Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers.

## Permission justification: tabCapture

Whole-tab mode: captures the current tab's sound (only after the person picks that mode) so it can be played back louder.

## Permission justification: offscreen

Whole-tab mode needs an offscreen document to play the captured tab audio through the volume boost.

## Permission justification: activeTab

The boost applies to the tab the person clicked the extension on.

## Permission justification: scripting

Video mode: injects the small script that routes that tab's video and audio through the volume boost.

## Permission justification: storage

Saves the boost level per site and the person's settings.
