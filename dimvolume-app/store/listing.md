## Full description
Is your phone's lowest brightness still too bright at night? Do the volume buttons jump from too quiet to too loud?

MES Dim & Volume fixes both:

SCREEN DIMMER
• A see-through dimming layer over every app, up to 80% darker, on top of the lowest backlight: far darker than the phone allows on its own.
• Optional warm, red or sepia night colour, with its own strength.
• Taps go straight through, so you use your phone as normal.
• −/+ buttons in the notification and a "Dim screen" Quick Settings tile.

FINE VOLUME
• 2, 4 or 8 small steps between each of your phone's volume steps.
• The app picks the phone step just above the level you want and turns the sound down the rest of the way.
• −/+ in the notification; the phone's own volume keys still work.

PRIVATE
• No ads, no account, no internet permission. Your settings stay on your phone.

Notes: Android does not let any app's layer pass 80% (so your taps keep working), and it hides such layers inside the Settings app for safety. Fine volume uses an audio effect on the phone's output that most phones support; if yours does not, the app says so and uses the phone's own steps.

## Foreground service (special use) declaration
The service keeps the user-enabled screen dimming overlay and fine volume effect running while other apps are in use, and shows the notification with their −/+ controls. It runs only while the user has the dimmer or fine volume switched on, and stops when both are off.
