Description:
Save the texts you paste again and again, and copy them with one tap.

Email replies, your address, links, hashtags, video descriptions, code snippets, signatures: paste or type them once, then tap a note whenever you need it and paste it anywhere.

* Tap a note to copy it, or "Paste & save" to keep what is on your clipboard
* Boards to keep things apart (work, social, school...)
* Pin your favourites, give notes colours and drag them into order
* Search across every board
* {date}, {time} and {weekday} are filled in when you copy
* Split a long text into separate notes: title, link, description, hashtags
* Show a note big on screen, or have it read aloud
* Bigger or smaller text, light and dark mode
* Back up everything to a file, or encrypted to your own Hive account

Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your texts stay on this phone and work without a connection.

Review notes (ASC): No account or sign-in. Type or paste a text into the box and tap "Save to this board"; tap "Copy" on a note to copy it to the clipboard (then paste it in any app). "Paste & save" reads the clipboard after the iOS paste prompt. More > "Add a few example texts" fills the board. Everything is stored on the device (no server). The optional "Hive backup" uses the Hive Keychain app (HiveAuth) to save an encrypted copy to the user's own Hive blockchain account; nothing else needs a login. No user-generated content is shared with other users.
