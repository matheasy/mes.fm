# App Review notes: MES Copy Text (paste into App Review Information > Notes)

1. Purpose and audience
MES Copy Text is a clipboard shelf for anyone who pastes the same texts often (email replies, addresses, links, hashtags, signatures). Made by Math Easy Solutions (mes.fm); it is the app version of https://mes.fm/copy-text.

2. How to use it
- No account or sign-in is needed.
- Type or paste text into the box at the top and tap "Save to this board".
- Tap "Copy" on a note: the text is on the clipboard; paste it in any app.
- "Paste & save" saves what is on the clipboard (iOS asks to allow the paste).
- More > "Add a few example texts" fills the board for a quick look.
- More > "Download this board (.txt)" / "Back up everything (.json)" open the share sheet (Save to Files etc.); "Restore from a backup" reads such a file.

3. External services
None are required. Everything is stored on the device. The optional "Hive backup" saves an encrypted copy of the texts to the user's own account on the Hive blockchain, approved in the separate Hive Keychain app (HiveAuth). It is not a social feature: nobody else can see or reply to the texts.

4. Regional differences
None. English only.

5. User-generated content
Texts are private to the device; nothing is shared with other users, so there is no content to moderate.
