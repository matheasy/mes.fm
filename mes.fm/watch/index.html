Watch YouTube, 3Speak, Rumble and Odysee Videos Without Distractions

Free distraction-free video player: paste YouTube, 3Speak, Odysee, Rumble, BitChute or X links and watch them on a plain page with no recommendations or comments. Up next list, save for later, resume where you stopped, speed up to 4x. Nothing is uploaded.

☁ Sync & backup

What to back up

Sync with your own cloud

One file, mes-watch.json, with your Up next, Saved, playlists, channels and history (and where you stopped). Your browser writes it straight to your own account; nothing goes through mes.fm. Pick the same place on your phone and it stays in step.

Back up to Hive

A permanent backup on your Hive account (Up next, Saved, playlists and channels; not history). Log in with Hive Keychain.

Watch videos without the distractions

MES Watch plays a video from YouTube, 3Speak, Odysee, Rumble, BitChute or X on a plain page: no recommended videos down the side, no comments, no autoplaying feed of other people's videos. Paste one link or a whole list, and the page plays them in order. It remembers where you stopped, so a long video picks up from the same spot next time, even after you close the tab.

What works with each site

YouTube
Played with YouTube's own player in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Speed from 0.25× to 2×, resume, and the next video starts by itself when one ends. Shorts and live links work too.
3Speak and Odysee
The page plays the video file straight from the site in a normal player, so any speed from 0.25× to 4×, resume and auto-next all work.
Rumble, BitChute and X
These play in the site's own small player, so the speed buttons and resume don't reach inside them. For Rumble use the Embed link (Share, then Embed): a normal rumble.com page link can't be played on another site, so the page offers to open it on Rumble instead.
Video files
A link straight to an .mp4, .webm or .mp3 file plays in the normal player, if the site that has the file allows it.

Playlists

Paste a YouTube playlist link (one with list= in it) and its videos are added, and kept under My playlists with the playlist's name. You can also build your own: press + List under the video to add it to a playlist, or + New playlist from Up next. Mix YouTube, 3Speak, Odysee and Rumble videos in the same playlist. YouTube sends at most 200 videos of a playlist this way.

Latest videos from any channel

Press + Channel and paste a YouTube channel link (youtube.com/@name) or just @name, or paste the channel link in the box above. It gets its own chip under Latest from showing its 15 newest uploads (newest first; tick Shorts to include them). Add them all to Up next, or keep them as one of your playlists. Your channels are kept in this browser only; × on a chip removes it.

Zoom and full screen

Pinch the video with two fingers (or Ctrl + scroll on a computer), and drag to move around, up to 400%. Double-tap goes to Fill and back; one tap plays or pauses. The player's own control bar at the bottom still works. With a mouse, the player is left alone at 100% (click, double-click and dragging the captions work as on YouTube): zoom with the small + / − / Fill strip on the right of the video, or the + and − keys, then drag to move around. The Zoom button (or Z) adds − / + buttons. Fill crops the black bars so the picture fills a wide phone screen. For zoom in full screen, use the page's own Full screen or Fill window button above the video rather than the player's own full-screen button.

Captions and 2× on a phone

The CC button under the video (and on the play bar while zoomed in) turns YouTube's captions on and off, in your language when the video has it. Use it when the picture is zoomed: the player's own CC button moves with the zoom and can't be pressed. Press and hold the picture to play at 2× while you hold, like the YouTube app, without YouTube's bar popping up.

Questions

Is anything sent to MES?

No. Your lists, history and resume points are kept in this browser only. The videos come straight from the sites that host them, the same as on those sites.

Can I see the comments?

Only if you want to: press 💬 Comments under the video (or C). It stays the way you leave it. YouTube comments come from YouTube (top comments first, Load more for the rest), 3Speak comments are the replies to its Hive post, and Odysee comments come from Odysee. Rumble, BitChute and X comments are only on their own sites. Comments are read-only here; to reply, open the video on its site.

Will these videos show up in my YouTube history?

Not while Private is ticked: the players are loaded without your sign-in cookies (YouTube's privacy-enhanced player, and a "credentialless" frame in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera), so YouTube, Rumble and BitChute don't know it is you. Untick it if you want them in your history.

Does YouTube still count the view?

Yes. It is YouTube's own player, so views, ads and the creator's earnings work as on youtube.com.

How do I move my list to another computer or phone?

Press ☁ Sync & backup and pick Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive or a file in a synced folder, then pick the same place on the other device: both stay in step from then on, including where you stopped in each video. Or press Copy a link to this list and open that link on the other device.

Can I keep several lists?

Yes: on the Up next tab press 💾 Save Up next as a playlist; it is kept under My playlists, and Up next can then be cleared for something else. Play any playlist again with ▶ (or + to add it to Up next).

Can I choose what is backed up?

Yes: ☁ Sync & backup → What to back up. Untick Up next, Saved, channels, history or any playlist, and it stays on this device only: it is not put in the cloud file or the Hive backup, and the copy on your other devices is left alone.

What about the tracking codes in shared links?

Links people share often carry a code saying who shared it (YouTube's si=, utm_…, Rumble's pub=, X's s=). MES Watch removes them from the links it keeps and copies. Tick Keep the sites' tracking codes to keep them.

Can I back up my lists to Hive?

Yes: ☁ Sync & backup → Back up to Hive, encrypted with your own memo key by default. It is permanent; "Add to mine" brings back anything you don't have.

Can I see my own videos from my computer here?

Use the MES Gallery for photos and videos saved on your computer or phone.
