A setup is a voice, a speed and a highlight style. Make one for each purpose (studying, quick listening, a person who likes it slow) and switch in the player or the toolbar popup. Changes save automatically.

Close the player when the reading ends

While the player is open: Space pause / resume, ← → previous / next sentence, ↑ ↓ speed, H highlighting on / off, M minimise, Esc close. Drag the player by its grip or any empty part to move it (double-click the grip to put it back). The start shortcut (Alt+Shift+R by default) can be changed at chrome://extensions/shortcuts (brave://extensions/shortcuts, edge://extensions/shortcuts).

The ◐ button darkens the page only while the player is open. Want every page dark? Try MES Dark Mode, our free extension (the ◐ button steps aside when it is on).

Google Docs, PDFs and Word files

Google DocsDocs draws its pages itself, so the extension reads the Doc's text from Google with your own login. Allowing it once makes this work for every Doc you can open, long ones included. Nothing goes through mes.fm.
PDFs and Word files on this computerThe browser's own PDF viewer cannot be read by extensions, and Word files just download. The extension opens them in its own reading page instead. For files on your computer, switch on Allow access to file URLs on the extension's details page (or simply pick the file in the reading page).
Read a PDF or Word file nowDrop or choose a .pdf, .docx or .txt file. It is read on this computer; nothing is uploaded.
