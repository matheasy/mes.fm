Let the extension open files on your computer

The browser shows PDFs from your computer in its own viewer, which extensions cannot read. To read this one here, switch on one setting once:

  1. Open the extension's details page: (or go to chrome://extensions, brave://extensions or edge://extensions and click Details under MES Read Aloud).
  2. Switch on Allow access to file URLs.
  3. Come back to this tab and press .
