Melissa Doi and the 1,613 Emergency 9/11 Calls

Article · September 16, 2026 · mirrored from the Hive blockchain and Telegram

I get tons of comments asking why the Melissa Doi 9/11 call was beeped out.

The 1,613 emergency phone calls on 9/11 were withheld by a court order, while families of victims fought to get the calls released.

But these had all the voices of civilians bleeped out, except for a few minutes of the Melissa Doi call, as it was played in the Zacarias Moussaoui (patsy) trial.
