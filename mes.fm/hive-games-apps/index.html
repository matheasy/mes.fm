New Hive Games & Apps on mes.fm: Chess, Billiards, Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four, Quantum-Safe Backups (Testers Wanted)

Post · November 16, 2026 · mirrored from the Hive blockchain

Over the last few weeks I've been adding Hive to more and more of the free apps on mes.fm. Some are games where every move is signed on the Hive blockchain, some use Hive to keep your stuff backed up. Everything runs in your browser, and you log in with Hive Keychain (the browser extension) or, without the extension, with the Keychain phone app (HiveAuth: scan a QR code or tap a button). Your keys never touch mes.fm.

Here's what's new. I'm looking for testers: please try them, try to break them, and tell me about bugs, ideas and requests in the comments.

♟️ MES Chess (2D and 3D)

https://mes.fm/chess

  • Play the computer (easy to expert), a friend on the same device, a guest online, or another Hive player: every move is a custom_json (a small piece of app data saved in a Hive transaction: a signed note on the blockchain that anyone can read), with time per move from 5 minutes to 3 days, and draw offers.
  • Normal chess, Chess960, King of the Hill and Three-check, plus typed-in positions. (Chess960 shuffles the back-row pieces at the start, King of the Hill is won by getting your king to the centre, and Three-check is won by giving check three times.)
  • 2D board or a 3D board with different sets (wood, marble, glass, tournament) and venues (chess club, tournament hall, park, home, space, and "video call fans").
  • Finished games can be posted to Hive as a PGN, tagged #chessbrothers. PGN (Portable Game Notation) is the standard plain-text format for chess games: the players, date and result, then every move written out (1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 ...). Any chess site or program (Lichess, Chess.com, ChessBase) can open a PGN and replay the game move by move.

@chessbrotherspro, I'd love it if you could try the 2D / 3D chess and tell me what you think. Any recommendations are welcome, and if you'd like a custom Chess Brothers venue in the 3D view (your colours, logo, a hall of your own), let me know and I'll build it in.

🎱 MES Billiards (8-ball, 9-ball, 10-ball and snooker)

https://mes.fm/billiards

  • Real physics: spin, cushions, pocket jaws, and jump shots.
  • 2D or 3D with cameras and rooms (bar, tournament arena, stadium, party club...).
  • Pull the cue back to shoot, play the computer (easy to expert), practice and trick-shot challenges, games without the aiming guide counted on their own.
  • Play online on Hive: each shot is a custom_json, and both sides re-run the same deterministic physics (the same shot always gives exactly the same result on every computer, down to the last bit), so nobody can fake a shot.
  • Quick links: 8-ball · 9-ball · Snooker

❌⭕ MES Tic-Tac-Toe

https://mes.fm/tic-tac-toe

  • 15 variants: classic, misère, wild, numerical, 4×4, gomoku, ultimate tic-tac-toe, 3D Qubic, Notakto, Order & Chaos and your own board size.
  • Computer, a friend, a guest online or a Hive player (open game or challenge an account). No server decides who won: both players' browsers replay the moves from the blockchain and check the result themselves.

🔴🟡 MES Connect Four

https://mes.fm/connect-four

  • Classic, Pop Out, misère, Connect 3 / 5 / 6, bigger boards and a three-player mode.
  • A strong computer that can show the score of every move. Hive games like tic-tac-toe, and results against the computer can be saved to Hive.

💣 MES Minesweeper

https://mes.fm/minesweeper

  • A daily board that's the same for everyone, and "no guessing" boards (boards you can always solve by logic alone, never a 50/50 guess).
  • Hive leaderboard: save a win to Hive and the server replays your moves from the blockchain before it accepts the time.

📊 MES Polls (also on iPhone)

https://mes.fm/polls

  • Make a poll in seconds, or publish it as a Hive post in the same format PeakD's polls use, so votes are counted from the chain (each vote is a custom_json on Hive, so anyone can recount them).

📋 Copy Text, Watch, Favourites, Workout Planner: backups on Hive, now quantum-safe

  • Copy Text (your clipboard shelf), Watch (YouTube / 3Speak / Odysee / Rumble without distractions, Hive replies under 3Speak videos), Favourites and the Workout Planner can all back up to Hive, encrypted by default.
  • New: a quantum-safe passphrase lock. Instead of locking the backup with your memo key (the Hive key used for private messages; like most of today's encryption it relies on maths that a large future quantum computer could break), it can be locked with AES-256 (the encryption banks and governments use; quantum computers only weaken it a little, not enough to break it) from a passphrase only you know. It works with the Keychain phone app too.
  • Why it matters, explained with simulations: Quantum Cryptography (with a "Your MES backups" tab), and a Quantum-Safe Website Checker that tests whether a site uses post-quantum encryption (newer kinds of encryption designed to stay safe even against quantum computers).

💰 MES Premium: no ads for 1 HBD a month

https://mes.fm/premium

  • Log in with Keychain and send 1 HBD a month (HBD = Hive Backed Dollars, Hive's coin that tracks the US dollar; a recurrent transfer is a payment Hive repeats by itself every month until you cancel it) or pay once, and mes.fm shows no ads in your browser. No account, no card.

🐞 Testers wanted: bugs, feedback and requests

These are new, so there will be bugs. The Hive parts especially need real people playing real games against each other (I've tested them mostly with a stand-in blockchain: a pretend copy of Hive inside the browser, so nothing real was signed). Things that help:

  • Start a Hive game of chess, billiards, tic-tac-toe or Connect Four and post the invite link in the comments so someone can join.
  • Try the Keychain phone app login (HiveAuth) on a computer without the extension, and on a phone.
  • Try a Hive backup with the passphrase lock, then load it back on another device.
  • Tell me what broke, on which browser / phone, and what you expected. Ideas and requests are welcome too.
