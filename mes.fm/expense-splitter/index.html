Expense Splitter: Split Trip and Group Costs Fairly

Free expense splitter for group trips: add who came and for how many nights, who paid for what, and split each bill equally, by nights, by shares or exact amounts, leaving out people who don't drink or weren't there. Kids at half share, several currencies, the fewest payments to settle up, and a share link. No sign-up, nothing uploaded.

    Split trip expenses fairly, even when people stayed different nights

    Going away with a group means one person books the cabin, another fills the tank, someone buys the wine and someone else pays for dinner. This splitter keeps the list and works out who owes whom, with the fewest payments to settle up.

    How to use it

    1. Add everyone. Set how many nights each person stayed. Tick No alcohol for people who don't drink, Kid for a half share, or make your own tag.
    2. Add each expense. Say who paid, the amount and how to split it: equally, by nights stayed, by shares, or with exact amounts for some people and the rest split equally.
    3. Leave people out. On the alcohol bill, choose Leave out: No alcohol. For a dinner on night 2, pick Night 2 and only the people who were there pay.
    4. Settle up. The page lists the payments, such as Alex pays Sam $42.10. Tap one when it is paid, or copy the list into your group chat.

    How the money is worked out

    Every amount is turned into whole cents in the trip's main currency. Each expense is split by the chosen weights, and the odd cents left over go to the people who have had the fewest odd cents so far, so nobody always pays the extra penny. Your balance is what you paid minus your share, plus payments you sent, minus payments you received. All balances add up to exactly zero.

    For settling up, the page looks for small groups of people whose balances cancel out on their own. Each group of k people needs only k − 1 payments, so finding the most such groups gives the fewest payments overall (exact for up to 14 people with something to pay or receive). You can also have everyone pay one person, who then pays out.

    Questions

    Is my trip private?
    Yes. Everything is saved only in this browser. A share link carries the trip inside the link itself; nothing is sent to mes.fm's servers, and the page's visit counter ignores the link's contents.
    Can I use several currencies?
    Yes. Give each expense its own currency and type the exchange rate you got. Everything is settled in the main currency.
    How does "by nights" work?
    Each person's share is their nights times their weight. Someone who stayed 1 night of 2 pays half as much as someone who stayed both. A kid counts as half.
    What if someone paid part of a bill for one person?
    Use Exact amounts: type that person's amount and leave the others blank, and the rest is split equally among them.
    Can I keep several trips?
    Yes, pick them from the Trip list. Download a backup to move them to another device.
