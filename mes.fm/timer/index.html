Sound and options

Online timer, stopwatch and alarm clock

This free online timer works on your computer and your phone, with no sign-up and nothing to install. Use it as a countdown timer for cooking, workouts, studying, tests and meetings, as a stopwatch with lap times, or as an alarm clock for a specific time of day.

What it can do

  • Several timers at once. Press + Add another timer and give each one a label: pasta, laundry, tea.
  • See the time in the browser tab. While a timer runs, its time is in the tab title, so you can work in another window and still check it at a glance.
  • Keeps counting. Reload the page or close the tab: a running timer or stopwatch carries on from the real clock, and a timer that finished while you were away says so.
  • Full screen. The expand button makes a huge, easy-to-read countdown for a classroom, a presentation or the gym wall.
  • Your sound, your volume. Friendly or annoying alarm, beeps, a chime or silence; once, three times or until you stop it. Use Test sound to check your speakers.
  • Share a timer. A link like mes.fm/timer?t=25m&label=Focus opens a ready-to-start 25-minute timer. Add &start=1 to start it at once.

Questions

Will the timer still work if I switch tabs?

Yes. It measures time from your computer’s clock, not by counting ticks, so it stays accurate in a background tab. Browsers may delay the alert by up to a second or so when a tab is hidden.

Why did I not hear the sound?

Browsers only allow sound after you have clicked something on the page, so press Start (or Test sound) at least once. Check your volume, and that the tab is not muted.
