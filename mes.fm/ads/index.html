Day by day

AdSense earnings (bars) and Google Ads spend (line), in the currency chosen at the top. Hover or tap a day for its numbers.

AdSense earningsGoogle Ads spend

Landing pages

Every page that earned or that ads point to. Spend and ad clicks are Google Ads' (the page the ad sends people to); earnings and page views are AdSense's for all visitors to that page, not only the ones from ads. "Came back" = ad visitors who returned later on their own. Per 1,000 views divides by all of a page's views, not only the ones ads brought: it shows whether the ads for a page pay for themselves.

Countries

Where ad clicks were bought and where AdSense earned. Earned per 1,000 views (RPM) is what a visitor from that country is worth.

Campaigns

Spend per Google Ads campaign, with clicks by device.

Devices

Sites in AdSense

Domains that showed your ad code. Only mes.fm is approved, so other sites earn nothing.

Do Google Ads visitors come back?

Visitors whose first visit was a Google Ads click, by landing page, and whether they came back later on their own (not through another ad). A day counts once however many pages are opened. Counted per browser, so a return on another device counts as not returning.
