Landing pages

Ad spend and clicks are from Google Ads (the page the ad sends people to); earnings and page views are AdSense's for all visitors to that page, not only the ones from ads. "Came back" = ad visitors who returned later on their own (first ad click in the chosen range).

Countries

Do Google Ads visitors come back?

Visitors whose first visit was a Google Ads click, by the page they landed on, and whether they came back later on their own. A day counts once however many pages are opened. Counted per browser, so a return on another device counts as not returning.
