Hutchison Effect: George Hathaway and John Alexander Discuss Crumbling Steel & Bent Molybdenum Rods

Video clip · September 1, 2026 · mirrored from the Hive blockchain
View on 3Speak ↗

Rare video and photos of George Hathaway and Col. John Alexander discussing steel samples that crumbled, fragmented, and turned into a black cloud (aka dustified), as well as the super-strong molybdenum rods being bent during the Hutchison Effect.

George shows the mild and case-hardened steel samples in his 1993 presentation. Later he shows the case-hardened steel sample and molybdenum rod in 2022, and recently emailed MES photos of the 3 rods he has in his possession.

Both steel rods are shown in George's 2009 paper, which includes a Scanning Electron Micrograph (SEM) image of the fractured surface.

John Alexander discussed this steel rod (which was also tested at BC Hydro), as well as PVC that was “pulverized and disappeared” and a steel file that glowed white and split apart without heat.

Alexander says he was surprised that John Hutchison was able to bend the molybdenum rods, which served as controls — no one had been able to bend them during his telekinesis (PK) investigations!
