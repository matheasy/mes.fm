#911Truth Part 26: 9/11 WTC Vehicle Massacre by xdesmond

Article · December 17, 2023 · mirrored from the Hive blockchain
View on 3Speak ↗

In #911Truth Part 26 I have uploaded a compilation of hundreds of photos of the many toasted, burnt, distorted, crushed, and mangled cars of 9/11. This compilation was produced by the YouTuber @xdesmond he has titled it WTC Vehicle Massacre. Note that the first person, that I am aware of, to point out the toasted cars was Dr. Judy Wood in her book Where Did The Towers Go? She refers to the cars as "toasted" to refer to them being damaged beyond repair, and to avoid connotations of conventional fire. The cars were destroyed in an unconventional method, some several blocks away from the World Trade Center, all while millions of unburnt paper were present. Many eye-witnesses reported spontaneous composition, "cars exploding", and even levitation! The closest explanation for what happened to the cars is the work of eccentric inventor John Hutchison in what is coined The Hutchison Effect. In fact, John even signed under oath in an affidavit that this was exactly the case! https://t.me/meslinks/16545
