External pages that also update in real time — handy for planning what to observe tonight.

  • TheSkyLive – Planets (positions, rise/set, visibility)
  • TheSkyLive – Interactive Planetarium
  • timeanddate.com – Sun & Moon for this location

The Sun and Moon figures are computed on your device with Astronomy Engine (an offline astronomy library). Place search and time-zone lookup use Open-Meteo's geocoding API; the 9 AM temperature comes from Open-Meteo's forecast. Choosing “Use my location” shares your coordinates with Open-Meteo for the forecast only — nothing is stored off your device. Values may differ by up to a minute (rise / set) or a degree or two (temperature) from weather.com and TheSkyLive, which use their own models.
