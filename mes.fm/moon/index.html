The Sun and Moon figures, the Moon-phase drawing and both charts are computed on your device with Astronomy Engine (an offline astronomy library). Place search and time-zone lookup use Open-Meteo's geocoding API; conditions come from Open-Meteo's forecast and air quality from its Copernicus (CAMS) feed. Choosing “Use my location” shares your coordinates with Open-Meteo for those lookups only — nothing is stored off your device. Values may differ by up to a minute (rise / set) or a degree or two (temperature) from weather.com and TheSkyLive, which use their own models.
