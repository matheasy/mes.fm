How to use the emoji copier

  • Click any tile to copy that emoji instantly — a toast confirms it, and it's ready to paste anywhere.
  • Click the small circle in a tile's top-right corner to Expand it into a large PNG with a transparent background — pick 512px, 1024px or 2048px, then Download or Copy image straight to your clipboard. Great for video and photo thumbnails instead of googling for a big emoji.
  • Click several emojis in a row to build a combo in the box at the bottom, then hit Copy all to grab the whole string at once.
  • Use the search box to jump straight to an emoji by name — try “crying”, “cat”, or “satellite”.
  • Your last several copies show up under Recently copied for quick reuse.

What emojis are included?

Smileys and happy faces, sad/crying/angry faces, hearts and love, hand gestures, monsters and fantasy faces, animals, weather, sun/moon/space (including satellites and rockets), food and drink, sports and activities, travel and places, everyday objects, common signs/symbols, and a handful of flags.

Why use this instead of my keyboard's emoji picker?

It works the same on any device, lets you search by full name instead of guessing an icon, and lets you build and copy several emojis as one string at once — plus nothing else offers a one-click large transparent PNG export for thumbnails.

Is anything sent to a server?

Nothing you type or copy ever leaves your device. The emoji list lives in this page's own JavaScript, copying happens via your browser's clipboard API, and the large PNG is drawn locally on a canvas. The one exception: expanding an emoji fetches its high-resolution artwork from the public Twemoji CDN (so the export looks sharp at any size, not just your device's own emoji font) — that's a request for public emoji artwork, not anything of yours.
