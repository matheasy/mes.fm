Overview ▼

View on 3Speak ↗

In this video I have uploaded a rare 2007 interview of John Hutchison in his Vancouver lab apartment by photographer Tom Sky and his Polish engineer uncle. John also runs a Hutchison Effect experiment live for them, which levitates a plastic can and aluminum foil in the back. I also include later emails from Tom saying he felt strangely ill after witnessing the Hutchison Effect. Tom told me that he recovered by "grounding in nature" via walking in sand and water at the beach – he felt electrical discharge leave his body! Tom may join a future MES Livestream with John soon, so stay tuned!

This is rare footage that shows John recording and playing back experiment footage live to observers, as well as showing an extended look at John when he is operating the many radio and electromagnetic frequency controls!
