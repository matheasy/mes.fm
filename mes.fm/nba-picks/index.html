Track your NBA standings and playoff predictions against the live table

MES NBA Picks checks your predicted final standings for the Eastern and Western Conference, the whole league (all 30 teams, 1 to 30) and the six divisions against the current NBA standings, and scores your playoff picks (second round, conference finals, NBA Finals and champion) as the series are played. It shows how many teams are in the exact spot, within one, two or three spots, in the right tier (top 6 playoff spot, 7th to 10th play-in, 11th to 15th lottery), plus a points score and a rank correlation, with a picture that joins each of your picks to where that team is now.

How the scores work

Exact counts the teams in the spot you picked. Within 1, 2 or 3 counts the teams that many spots or fewer away. Right tier counts the teams in the same group you put them in. Right side of the line checks only whether you had a team above or below the key cut (the top 10 of a conference, the top 16 of the league, the division winner). Points gives 3 for an exact spot, 2 for one off and 1 for two off. Closeness is 100% minus the spots you are off as a share of the worst possible order. Spearman’s ρ is the usual rank correlation: 1 is the same order, −1 the reverse. Playoff picks earn 1 point for each team you send to the second round that gets there, 2 for each conference finalist, 4 for each Finals team and 8 for the champion.

Questions

Where do the standings and results come from?

From ESPN’s public standings and scoreboard feeds, refreshed about every 10 minutes, in ESPN’s published order (which applies the NBA tie-breakers once teams have played enough games).

Why do tied teams share their spots?

In the first weeks many teams have the same record, and their order is a coin flip of tie-breakers. With the box ticked a pick counts as exact anywhere inside the group of spots the tied teams cover.

Can I fix my picks after the season starts?

Yes, but the leaderboard shows how many games had been played when you last changed them, and a tie on the board goes to whoever picked earlier. Playoff picks close for the board when the playoffs start.

Is anything about me stored?

Your sets of picks stay in this browser (download a backup to move them). Only if you post to the leaderboard are the name you chose and your picks stored, with a private key in your browser that lets you change or remove them.
