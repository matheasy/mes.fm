Falling Blocks: Play Free Online, Sprint, Ultra, Races and a Hive Leaderboard

Play a free falling-block puzzle game in your browser: Marathon, Sprint 40 lines, Ultra 2 minutes and Zen, modern rules (7-bag, wall kicks, hold, ghost piece, T-spins, combos, back-to-back), keyboard, touch and gamepad controls, 2D or 3D, a daily race on the same pieces for everyone, live races against a friend or the computer, and a Hive-verified leaderboard.

Mode

Controls and settings

Keyboard: ← → move, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop, ↑ or X rotate right, Z or Ctrl rotate left, A rotate 180°, C or Shift hold, P or Esc pause, N new game, R restart, M sound, V 2D / 3D.

Touch: drag sideways to move, tap to rotate (left half turns left, right half turns right), drag down slowly for a soft drop, flick down to drop, flick up to hold. In 3D, drag with the mouse (or two fingers, or one finger after tapping 🎥 Turn view) to look all the way round the well; the mouse wheel or a pinch zooms, and double-click or ↺ Front view goes back.

Gamepad: d-pad moves (up = hard drop, down = soft drop), A rotates right, B rotates left, X rotates 180°, Y or a shoulder button holds, Start pauses.

Race someone live

You both get the same pieces. Lines you clear send garbage rows to the other player (2 lines send 1, 3 send 2, 4 send 4, T-spins more). First to clear 40 lines wins, or the one still standing.

Falling blocks, free in your browser

Seven shapes made of four squares fall into a well 10 squares wide. Move and turn each one so they fill whole rows: a full row disappears and everything above it drops. Clear four rows with one long piece for the most points. The game ends when the stack reaches the top. No install, no account, and it works with a keyboard, a touch screen or a gamepad, in 2D or 3D.

Modes

Marathon
Start at any level; every 10 lines the pieces fall faster, up to level 20 where they appear on the floor. Clear 150 lines for the end. Highest score wins.
Sprint 40
Clear 40 lines as fast as you can. The clock stops on the 40th line.
Ultra 2 min
Two minutes at a steady speed: score as many points as you can. Quads, T-spins and back-to-backs are worth far more than single lines.
Zen
No speed-up, no clock and no game over: when the stack reaches the top the well empties and you carry on.
Daily race
A Sprint 40 on the day's seed, the same pieces for everyone in the world, with its own leaderboard per UTC day.
Race
Live against another person (share a link or join an open race) or the computer. You get the same pieces; the lines you clear push garbage rows into the other well.

The rules the page uses

They follow the modern standard most falling-block games share: the pieces come in shuffled bags of all seven, so you never wait long for a long piece; turning a piece next to a wall or the floor tries a few nudges ("wall kicks") before it gives up; you can keep one piece aside with Hold (once per piece); the ghost shows where the piece will land; and a piece touching the floor waits half a second before it locks, which you can stretch by moving it (up to 15 times).

Scoring

Lines
1 row 100, 2 rows 300, 3 rows 500, 4 rows (a quad) 800 points, times the level.
T-spins
Turning the T piece into a tight slot (three of the four corners around its centre filled) scores 400, with 1, 2 or 3 rows 800, 1,200 or 1,600, times the level. A "mini" T-spin scores less.
Back-to-back
A quad or a T-spin with lines right after another one (with no ordinary clear in between) scores 1.5 times.
Combo
Clearing rows with piece after piece adds 50 × combo × level.
Perfect clear
Leaving the whole well empty adds a big bonus.
Drops
1 point per row of soft drop, 2 per row of hard drop.

Saving to Hive and the leaderboard

After a Sprint, Ultra, Marathon or daily race you can save the result to the Hive blockchain with "Save to Hive". It sends one small public message (a custom_json operation with the id mes_blocks) from your Hive account, signed with your posting key through Hive Keychain. It holds the seed, your time, lines, score and every move you made with its timing, so mes.fm can replay the game exactly and check it before it goes on the leaderboard. It costs no HIVE (a little of your free Resource Credits), and like everything on Hive it is public and permanent.

Questions

Is this Tetris?
No. Tetris is a trademark of The Tetris Company, and this page has nothing to do with it. MES Falling Blocks is our own game in the same old genre of falling-block puzzles, with its own code, art and sounds.
What are DAS and ARR?
When you hold left or right, the piece moves once, waits the "auto-repeat delay" (DAS), then moves again every "repeat rate" (ARR) milliseconds. ARR 0 sends it straight to the wall. Lower numbers are faster; good players use around 100 ms and 0-10 ms.
Why can't I save a Zen game?
Zen has no end and no score race, so there is nothing to rank.
Can I play on a phone?
Yes: drag to move, tap to turn, flick down to drop, or use the buttons under the board. Fill window gives the board the whole screen.
Does 3D change the game?
No. It is the same game drawn with depth and light; you can switch between 2D and 3D in the middle of a game, and turn the camera all the way round while you play.
