In this video, I go over the end-of-chapter Problems Plus questions and solve 7 very advanced problems on vector functions. Three of the questions involve launching a projectile either on a flat, inclined, or declined plane. One question is on curvature, one on finding the equation of a plane, another is on rolling a ball off a table, and my favorite one is on finding the axial distance covered when wrapping a cable of known length around a spool of known diameter. Buckle up for 5 hours of mathematics!

(c) Suppose the projectile is fired up an inclined plane whose angle of inclination is θ.

Show that, in order to maximize the (uphill) range, the projectile should be fired in the direction halfway between the plane and the vertical.
