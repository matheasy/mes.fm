Hutchison Effect: Plastic Can Levitates and Video is Played Back LIVE

Video clip · September 22, 2026 · mirrored from the Hive blockchain

In this video I clip out the clips from the 2007 Tom Sky footage of the Hutchison Effect to show John playing back live footage he recorded with his camera of a levitating can that Tom and his uncle missed in their footage. This is a rare, and possibly only, find, as all other available footage show the antigravity and jellification effects after their tape recording had been published in various media outlets. I believe George Hathaway gave John this camera, which was placed on a tripod filming the target area.

I have also included bonus footage at the end of, also rare, footage of John Hutchison in the operating room of his Vancouver apartment lab!
