Simulators

Free physics and science simulators that run in your browser: black holes, orbits, fluids, quantum computers, the heart, neurons, fractals and more.

Calculators Tools Games Simulators Mobile Apps Extensions All apps

Space, time & relativity

Atomic Clock Simulator See how atoms keep time: lock a quartz clock to caesium, tune a fountain, race clocks against each other and compare their stability.
Black Hole Simulator Bend light around a black hole, find its shadow and photon sphere, follow orbits that precess or plunge, and see a ray-traced picture with a glowing disk.
Caesium Fountain Clock A 3D fountain atomic clock: cool a ball of atoms, toss it up through the microwave cavity and see why throwing higher stops helping.
Faster-Than-Light Shadow Simulator Swing a torch beam or move a finger and watch the spot or shadow on the Moon outrun light, with true light-travel delay. See why no message rides on it.
Metric Tensor Explorer Type or pick a metric (Schwarzschild, de Sitter, a wormhole...) and get the Christoffel symbols, Riemann, Ricci and Einstein tensors, plus geodesics drawn on curved surfaces.
Michelson-Morley Experiment Simulator Turn an interferometer through an aether wind, watch the fringes shift, compare the 1881 and 1887 experiments and calculate the expected shift.
Orbit Simulator Launch planets by dragging, watch real gravity with many bodies, see Kepler's laws and equal areas, try Halley's comet, the Moon and a gravity assist, and compute orbit speeds and transfers.
Redshift & Blueshift Simulator Why light changes colour: near and far from a heavy mass, moving sources, a spectrum shifter and the expanding universe, with real numbers.
Space Shuttle Simulator Fly the Shuttle around a 3D Earth: plan burns, raise and tilt the orbit, catch the ISS or Hubble, then de-orbit and glide down to the runway. Plus why it could never reach the Moon.
Time Dilation Simulator How much would you age at close to the speed of light? Watch a traveller's clock, heartbeat and ageing slow down, see the light clock that explains why, fly the twin paradox at a steady 1 g, and calculate it.

Light, fields & quantum

Electromagnetic Fields Simulator Place charges and wires and see field lines, Gauss's law and Ampère's law, steer a particle through E and B fields, and watch Maxwell's equations make waves reflect, refract and diffract.
Ground Rod & Earthing Simulator Find a ground rod's resistance for your soil, see why a rod doesn't trip a breaker, model lightning step voltage and static discharge, and read the grounding rules for your region.
Hologram Simulator Record interference fringes live, play the hologram back with real wave optics, see parallax and the twin image, compare hologram types and compute a phase hologram of your drawing.
Hutchison Effect Simulator Lay out John Hutchison's lab of Tesla coils, Van de Graaff, RF and spark gaps, see where the fields cross and watch samples levitate, bend like jelly, fracture and glow.
Light & Metamaterials Lab Negative refraction, the flat and perfect lens, a live Maxwell simulation of a metamaterial, diffraction gratings and ten things light can do.
Light in a Medium Simulator Paint glass, water or any refractive index and watch a live light wave slow down, bend, focus in a lens or curve like a mirage. Compare it with rays.
Magnetic Isopotentials Drag and turn bar magnets and see their flux lines and magnetic isopotentials at once, with compasses, horseshoe and Halbach examples.
Maxwell's Vortices A wire bent into a loop becomes a magnet, the electron's magnetic moment, Maxwell's 1861 spinning cells and idle wheels carrying currents and waves, and two magnets attracting or repelling.
Photoelectric Effect Simulator Shine light on a metal and watch electrons escape. Threshold, stopping voltage, graphs, and a lab to measure Planck's constant.
Quantum Computer Simulator Drag gates onto up to 10 qubits and watch the state vector and Bloch sphere step by step: Bell, teleportation, Grover, QFT and Shor examples, shots and OpenQASM.
Quantum Cryptography Send a key with photons (BB84), watch Shor's algorithm factor and Grover's search find, see what is quantum-safe, and run real post-quantum ML-KEM (Kyber).
Quantum Lab Watch a wave packet tunnel through a barrier, build double-slit stripes one particle at a time, run a Bell test on entangled pairs, send atoms through Stern-Gerlach magnets and explore hydrogen orbitals.
Quaternion Lab Multiply i, j, k and watch a 3D hand half-turn, see why i² = −1, compare SLERP with Euler angles on a robot arm, convert rotations and see the 720° spinor turn.
Spin Simulator Neutrons that must turn 720° to come back, the Einstein-de Haas effect (flip a magnet, the iron turns) and the Barnett effect (spin iron, it magnetizes).
Spin Wave Field A double-slit pattern forms in a crowd that waves with the wave, a 360° spin flipper swaps bright and dark stripes (720° brings them back), and a 3D wave field with tracer particles.
Spin-½ Lab Dirac's belt trick in 3D: one turn tangles the ribbons, two turns untangle them, plus five pictures of why spin ½ needs 720°, a phase-coloured torus and an elastic sheet that lies flat only after 720°.

Motion, materials & building

3D Fluid Simulator Real 3D smoke you can rotate: rising plumes, smoke rings that collide, wind past a sphere. Navier–Stokes solved live in your browser.
Brick Builder (3D building bricks) Build with virtual bricks in 3D, start from example builds for kids and bridges, test how much weight your build holds and follow the steps with real bricks.
Bridge Builder Design a truss, arch or cable bridge, check every member, auto-size it for the lowest cost, then drive trucks over it, blow wind and shake it with an earthquake.
Car Sandbox Drive cars, trucks and a lunar rover in 3D on a test track, the Moon, Mars or Ceres: jumps, a loop, crashes, computer cars, loose objects and any gravity you like.
Crash Physics Simulator Fire anything at anything: pick the material, speed and plate thickness and see if it bounces, sticks or goes through. Crash cars, drop eggs, read the g-forces.
Double Pendulum Drag two swinging arms into place and watch chaos: a fan of copies that drift apart, how fast errors grow, and a fractal map of how long each start takes to flip.
Fluid Simulator Navier–Stokes in 2D, live: vortex streets behind a cylinder, hot plumes, swirling layers. Stir it, block it, change the Reynolds number.
Gyroscope & Spinning Top Simulator Precession, nutation, a sleeping top, tops that rise by friction, a tippe top that flips, a gyroscope on ice, and the MES / Owen Liang Gyrotop experiments next to the textbook model.
Lüders Band Simulator Pull a mild steel bar and watch the yield band run along it at 55° while the force stays flat. Notches, machine stiffness, strain ageing.
Stunt Jump Simulator Jump cars, bikes and monster trucks over gaps and canyons with real suspension physics: airtime, rotation, landing g-force and the speed window for a clean landing.
Tensile Test Simulator Pull a bar until it breaks and watch the stress-strain curve, necking and toughness.
Voids & Ductile Fracture Simulator Watch microscopic voids grow and link inside a necking bar until it cracks from the middle: cup and cone, dimples, triaxiality.

Body, brain & AI

German New Medicine Explorer Dr. Hamer's two-phase chart with a draggable marker, organs by germ layer and brain relay, a describe-your-symptoms reader, the 5 Biological Laws and a glossary.
Heart Simulator An animated heart synced to a Wiggers diagram, pressure-volume loops with disease presets, an ECG rhythm trainer with calipers and a hemodynamics calculator.
Letter and Digit Recognition Train a small network to read digits and letters, draw a character and see what it thinks, test it on unseen fonts, and teach it your handwriting.
Neural Network Playground Train a real neural network in your browser: pick the data, layers and activation, and watch the decision boundary, the loss and every hidden neuron.
Neuron Simulator The Hodgkin-Huxley action potential, nerve conduction with myelin and MS, EPSP summation, the neuromuscular junction and a Nernst / Goldman calculator.
Toy Language Model Simulator See how a language model works in miniature: tokens, next-word probabilities, temperature, word vectors and attention.

Fractals & math

Buddhabrot Explorer Watch the Buddhabrot fill in live: millions of random Mandelbrot orbits piled into a glowing figure. Try the three-colour Nebulabrot and the Anti-Buddhabrot.
Julia Set Explorer Drag a point on a map of the Mandelbrot set and watch its Julia set change live. Walk c around the cardioid, zoom and save pictures.
Mandelbrot Explorer Zoom into the Mandelbrot set, see the Julia set of any point, trace orbits and try the Burning Ship, Tricorn and Multibrot fractals.
