Questions

Will it unlock at midnight?
That is the usual pattern for PlayStation digital games, region by region. Rockstar has not confirmed an hour, and Xbox has often unlocked games at one moment worldwide instead. If Rockstar announces a time, this page will change to it.
When does preloading start?
November 10, 2026 for digital copies. Preloading lets you download the game ahead of time so it is ready the moment it unlocks.
Can I get a reminder?
Yes: Set a reminder opens the MES Reminder with the unlock time filled in, or add it to Google Calendar or any calendar app with the .ics file (it carries a 15-minute alarm).
Can I put it on a big screen?
Yes: Fill window (W) makes the countdown fill the browser window and Full screen (F) fills the whole screen.
