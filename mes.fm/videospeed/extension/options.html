MES Video Speed Controller

A setup is a full set of shortcuts and preferences. Make one for each person or purpose (watching lectures, music, a fast review setup), switch between them here or from the toolbar popup, and even pin a setup to a site. Changes save automatically.

Shortcuts

Click a key box and press the key you want (Backspace clears it). Ctrl, Alt and Cmd combinations are left alone, and shortcuts are ignored while you type in a text box. The amount is the step for speed changes, the seconds for rewind and advance, or the speed to jump to. A marker is remembered per video until the page closes.
