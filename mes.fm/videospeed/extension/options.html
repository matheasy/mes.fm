MES Video Speed Controller

Settings are saved as you change them. Free, private and built by MES.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click a box, then press the key you want. Press Backspace in a box to clear it. Shortcuts only work when the page is not asking you to type, and Ctrl / Alt / Cmd combinations are left alone.

Speed

Press and hold the speed label on a video to play at the hold speed while you keep the button down, and drag left or right to change it (0.1 steps up to 4.0, then whole steps up to 10.0). Letting go puts your speed back.
