MES Archive settings

Sites to archive on

Tick the sites that open when you click the toolbar button, press Alt+Shift+A (change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts) or use the right-click menu.

Add your own archive site

Use %s (or {u}) where the page address goes, e.g. https://archive.example/save?url=%s. {raw} inserts it un-encoded.

Options

MES Archive by mes.fm/archive. Free, no accounts, no tracking. Saved pages on public archives can be seen by anyone: do not archive private pages.
