Description:
Thirteen free health, food and training calculators in one app. Pick a tile, type your numbers, and see the result with the formula shown.

BODY
* BMI Calculator: BMI, WHO or Asian scale, healthy weight range, history chart
* Body Fat Calculator: U.S. Navy tape method, lean mass, goal weight
* Ideal Weight: Devine, Robinson, Miller and Hamwi next to the healthy BMI range
* Waist-to-Hip Ratio: WHR, waist-to-height and body shape

FOOD AND WATER
* BMR and TDEE: daily calories to maintain, lose or gain, four equations compared
* Macro Calculator: protein, carbs and fat in grams for six splits
* Protein Intake: grams per day for your goal, per-meal chart and a food stepper
* Water Intake: daily target plus a drink tracker with streaks

TRAINING
* Heart Rate Zones: five zones, Karvonen option and a live pulse check
* Calories Burned: 85 activities, weekly totals and the 150-minute guideline
* Running Pace: pace, time or distance, splits and race predictions
* One-Rep Max: seven formulas, percent table and a plate loader

SLEEP
* Sleep Calculator: bedtimes and wake-up times in 90-minute cycles

Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device and works offline.

These are general estimates, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor, dietitian or qualified trainer before changing your diet, training or sleep.

Review notes (ASC): No account or sign-in. Every calculator is local; the app makes no network requests except when the user taps a link (opens mes.fm in the browser). Each tool shows its formula and links its sources (WHO, CDC, NIH, ACSM) at the bottom. Health disclaimer is in the app footer.
