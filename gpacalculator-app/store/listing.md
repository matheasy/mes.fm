Description:
What is my GPA? Type each course's grade as a letter (A, B+, C-) or a percentage, add its credits, and your GPA appears instantly.

* 4.0 and 4.33 scales, letters and percentages mixed freely
* Credit-weighted GPA per term, plus a cumulative GPA across all your terms
* Weighted GPA option: honors +0.5, AP / IB +1.0
* Add your GPA and credits from before to get a true cumulative GPA
* What GPA do I need next? Enter the cumulative GPA you want and the credits you will take
* Several terms side by side, with an all-terms summary
* Copy a link to move your terms to another phone or computer
* Light and dark theme, adjustable text size
* Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device and works offline.

Results are estimates from the numbers you enter. Schools differ on letter-grade points, A+ values and rounding, so check your school's own scale before relying on a result.
