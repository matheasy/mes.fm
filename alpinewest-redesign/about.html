About

Hi, I’m Karolina.

I’m the photographer and filmmaker behind Alpine West Creative. I photograph places the way guests remember them: the light on a morning terrace, the first sip at the bar, the quiet moment before dinner.

My background is in graphic design, so every shoot starts with the story a property wants to tell and how the images will be used, not just a list of rooms.

I’m based in Vancouver, British Columbia, and travel for assignments. Recent work has taken me to Mallorca, Puerto Vallarta and Calgary.

How I work.

Feeling first

I look for the moments that make someone want to book: people enjoying the place, not just the architecture.

Natural light

Warm, honest light and a cinematic eye, edited to stay true to how the property looks.

Made to be used

Images and films planned for your website, campaigns and social channels from the start.
