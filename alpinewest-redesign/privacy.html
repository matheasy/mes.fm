Privacy

Your privacy.

Last updated November 17, 2026.

No cookies, no tracking across sites

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or social-media pixels.

Page-view counts

When a page loads, a small anonymous count is recorded: which page was viewed, the address of the page you came from (for example a Google search), and a rough device type (computer, phone or tablet) worked out from your browser. Your IP address and anything else that could identify you are not stored. These counts only show which pages people find useful.

Messages you send

If you use the inquiry form, your name, email address and message are sent to us by email (through the form service Web3Forms) so we can reply. They are not shared with anyone else or used for marketing.
