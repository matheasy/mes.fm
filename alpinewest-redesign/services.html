How a project comes together.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    Tell us about your property, the season and what the images are for.

  2. 02

    Plan

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Delivery

Every project is quoted individually.

Questions

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. We’re based in Vancouver, British Columbia, and take assignments across Canada and internationally. Travel is included in the quote.

Can you provide models?

We can arrange talent for lifestyle scenes or work with your guests and staff. We’ll talk it through when planning.

How can we use the images?

Usage rights are agreed up front and set out in the quote, whether for your website, social media, print or paid campaigns.

Can photography and film be done on the same visit?

Yes, and it’s usually the most efficient way to work: one visit, one plan, both formats.
