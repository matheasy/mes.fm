About

Hi, I’m Karolina.

I photograph places the way guests remember them: the light on a morning terrace, the first sip at the bar, the quiet moment before dinner. With a background in graphic design, I plan every shoot around the story a property wants to tell.

Based in Vancouver, I travel for assignments across Canada and around the world.
