Description:
What grade do I need on my final exam to get the course grade I want? Type your current grade, what the final is worth and your target, and the answer appears instantly.

* Needed on the final: the exact score you need, or "already safe" / "out of reach"
* Two ways in: enter your current grade, or list every assignment with its score, points possible and weight
* What-if slider: see the course grade you would get for any final score
* Targets table: what you need for every goal from 40% to 100%, with common US letter labels
* Several courses at once, each with its own target, plus an overview of all of them
* Copy a link to move your courses to another phone or computer
* Light and dark theme, adjustable text size
* Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device and works offline.

Results are estimates from the numbers you enter. Your school's syllabus decides weighting, rounding and dropped scores, so check it before relying on a result.
