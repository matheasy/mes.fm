MES Upscale is a free AI picture upscaler by Math Easy Solutions (mes.fm). No account, no ads, no purchases, no sign-in.

Who it is for: anyone who wants a small or blurry picture bigger and sharper (photos, screenshots, logos, drawings, anime).

How to use it:
1. Tap "Choose pictures" (photo library) or "Try a sample" for a built-in test picture.
2. Pick what is in the picture (Photo / Drawing / Pixel art) and how big (2x, 3x, 4x, 8x or a width).
3. Tap "Upscale all". The AI models (Real-ESRGAN, BSD-3-Clause) are built into the app and run on the device; nothing is uploaded.
4. The before / after compare opens. Tap "Save" > "Save to Photos" (asks for permission to add photos), "Share..." or "Save to Files".

External services: none. The models and the AI engine (onnxruntime-web) are bundled, so the app works offline.
Links in the menu open mes.fm pages in the browser. No regional differences.
