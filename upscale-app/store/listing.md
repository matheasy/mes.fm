## Description (both stores)

MES Upscale makes small pictures bigger and sharper with AI, right on your phone.

Pick one or many pictures, choose how big (2×, 3×, 4×, 8× or an exact width like 1080p, 4K or 8K) and tap Upscale. The AI adds real-looking detail and clean edges instead of the blur of a plain resize.

• Photo & everyday: photos, screenshots, logos and scans (Real-ESRGAN general).
• Drawing, anime & cartoon: a model made for line art and flat colours.
• Pixel art: keeps every pixel crisp.
• AI detail slider: mix in a plain resize if a result looks too smooth.
• Compare before and after with a slider or side by side, and zoom in.
• Save to Photos / your gallery, or share to any app, as PNG, JPEG or WebP.

Private by design: the AI models are built into the app and run on your phone (on the graphics chip when it can), so it works offline and nothing is uploaded. No account, no ads, no tracking, no watermark.

Made by Math Easy Solutions (mes.fm). The same upscaler runs in any browser at mes.fm/upscale.
