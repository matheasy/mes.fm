The agnostic poses as fair, impartial, and balanced. See how many fallacies you can find in it. Here are a few obvious ones: First the agnostic allows the arbitrary into the realm of human cognition [like your non-flight 77 fixed-wing plane disappearing through a 16-foot hole in the Pentagon].
