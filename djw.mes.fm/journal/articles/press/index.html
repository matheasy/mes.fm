Appeal Filed in 9/11 Qui Tam Case of Dr Judy Wood
1 August 2008
 Dr. Judy Wood has filed an appeal regarding the decision regarding her Qui Tam Case against NIST Contractors who helped to compile the 9/11 WTC Reports

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

PRLog (Press Release) Aug 01, 2008 Contact:  Jerry Leaphart, Attorney for Dr. Judy Wood

Dr. Judy Wood has filed an appeal regarding the decision handed down by Hon. George B. Daniels on June 26, 2008. Her appeal is now pending in the U.S. Court of Appeals, Second Circuit. Dr. Wood asserts that Judge Daniels erred in ordering the dismissal of  her case and will detail the reasons in support of her appeal in an appellate  brief that will be filed in accordance with a schedule that will be issued by  the Court of Appeals. No additional statements about the appeal are  anticipated at this time.  Further, it should be noted that  there is only one official source of information about the work and the findings  of Dr. Judy Wood.  That source is her website:  http://www.drjudywood.com/  (She has only this one website.)
