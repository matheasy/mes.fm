Description:
Make a poll in seconds, share one short link, and watch the votes come in live.

Your friends, family, team or class vote in any browser: they don't need the app, an account or an email. Perfect for picking a restaurant, a movie, a meeting time, or settling any group decision.

* Up to 20 options, one pick or several
* Close it yourself, after a set time, or at a date and time
* Show results always, after people vote, or only when it closes
* Allow vote changes or not
* Live bars and a donut chart, updated every few seconds
* QR code and share sheet for posting the link anywhere
* "My polls": every poll you made, with close and delete
* Optional: publish a poll on the Hive blockchain instead

Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. Polls are not listed anywhere or shown in search engines. Each phone or browser can vote once; voters are never identified.

Review notes (ASC): No account or sign-in. Tap "Create the poll" to make one; open the link it shows (or the poll in "My polls") to vote and see live results. Polls are stored on mes.fm (our own server, https://mes.fm/api/poll) and deleted 60 days after closing. The Hive option is optional and uses the Hive Keychain app; nothing else needs a login. No user-generated content is listed publicly: a poll can only be opened by people who have its link, and the maker can delete it.
