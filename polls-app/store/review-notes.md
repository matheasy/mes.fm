1. Purpose and audience
MES Polls lets anyone make a quick poll and share one short link; the people they send it to vote in any web browser, without the app or an account, and the maker watches the results come in live. It is for friends, families, teams and classes making a group decision (where to eat, a meeting time, which option to pick). The problem it solves: group chats make it hard to count opinions, and most poll sites require sign-up or show ads.

2. How to use the main features (no account, no sign-in, no sample files needed)
- Make a poll: type a question and at least two options, choose the settings (one or several picks, when it closes, when results are shown, vote changes), tap "Create the poll".
- Share: the app shows the poll's link (mes.fm/v/<id>) with Copy, Share and a QR code.
- Vote and see results: open the poll from "My polls" (or the link in any browser), pick an option, tap Vote. The bars and the donut chart update every few seconds.
- Manage: "My polls" lists every poll made on this phone, with Close and Delete.
- Optional: the Hive choice under "Where should the poll live?" publishes the poll as a post on the Hive blockchain instead. This needs the user's own Hive account through the Hive Keychain app; nothing else in the app uses a login.

3. User-generated content and moderation (guideline 1.2)
Poll questions and options are written by users. How we keep this safe:
- Rules: the create form says "Keep it respectful: no hate, harassment, sexual content, threats or other people's private information", linked to our rules at https://mes.fm/privacy-policy#poll-rules.
- Filtering: polls whose text contains a slur are refused by the server when they are made.
- Reporting: every poll someone else made has a "Report" button (reasons: spam or scam, hate or harassment, sexual content, violence or threats, private information, something else). Reports go to our server and into a review queue we check. A poll reported from 3 different networks is hidden from everyone at once until we review it, and polls that break the rules are deleted.
- Blocking: there are no accounts or profiles, so there is no user to block. Instead, reporting a poll hides it on that device ("Also hide this poll on this device", on by default), and the person never sees it again unless they choose "Show it anyway".
- Polls are never listed publicly or shown in search results; only people who have a poll's link can open it. The maker can close or delete a poll at any time, and every poll is deleted 60 days after it closes.
- Contact for urgent reports: https://mes.fm/contact

4. External services
- mes.fm (our own server, https://mes.fm/api/poll) stores polls and votes. Voters are counted per device with a random ID, stored only as a hash; no names, emails or accounts.
- Hive blockchain (optional, only for the Hive choice under "Where should the poll live?"): public Hive API nodes, and the Hive Keychain app for signing.
No analytics, no ads, no payments, no AI services.

5. Regional differences
None. The app works the same in every region.
