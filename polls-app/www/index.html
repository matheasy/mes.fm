    Open a poll

    A free poll maker with no sign-up

    Type a question and a few answers, press Create the poll, and share the short link (mes.fm/v/...). Anyone who opens it can vote with one tap and see the results as bars and a pie chart, updated live every few seconds while the poll is open. Use it in a group chat, a class, a livestream, a club or a family decision.

    Choices you can make

    One option or several
    A single-choice poll takes one answer; a multiple-choice poll lets people tick up to the number you choose.
    When voting ends
    Keep it open until you close it yourself, close it after a set time (15 minutes, a day, a week ...), or at a date and time you pick. An open poll closes by itself after a year.
    Who sees the results
    Everyone at any time, only people who have voted (so nobody just follows the crowd), or nobody until voting ends.
    Changing a vote
    Off by default; turn it on if people may change their mind while the poll is open.

    How one vote per person works (and its limits)

    There are no accounts, so the page gives each browser a random number and lets it vote once per poll. A few votes are allowed from the same network (a school, an office or a phone network share one internet address), and then that network is full for that poll. This stops casual repeat voting. It cannot stop someone who clears their browser data or uses several devices or networks, so do not use these polls for anything that needs a real election.

    Polls on Hive

    Choose On Hive to publish the poll as a post from your Hive account in the open polls format that PeakD, Ecency and HiveHub use, so it shows up as a poll there too. Hive accounts vote with Hive Keychain (a free "custom_json" operation signed with the posting key, no fee). This page counts the votes itself: it asks PeakD's open polls node who voted, then reads each vote from its block on the Hive blockchain and applies the poll's rules (votes after the end time do not count, an account's latest vote wins unless changes are off, the minimum account age). You can open any Hive poll here by pasting its PeakD link.

    Privacy

    The poll (question, options, settings) and the vote counts are stored on our server until 60 days after the poll closes, then they are deleted. We keep which browser number picked which option (so it cannot vote twice) but never your name, and your internet address only as a scrambled code used to count votes per network. Your list of polls and the keys to manage them stay in your own browser. See the privacy policy.

    Questions

    Can I edit a poll after making it?
    No, so nobody can change the question under the voters. Close or delete it and make a new one.
    Can I see who voted for what?
    No. Only the totals are shown, to everyone including you.
    Why does it say my network already voted?
    Each poll takes a few votes per internet address. On shared Wi-Fi, try your phone's mobile data.

    Counting down to the result? Make a reminder link for the closing time. Picking a winner at random instead? Try the random number generator or the dice roller.

    How it works. A poll is saved on mes.fm and gets a short link (mes.fm/v/…) that anyone can open in a browser to vote: your voters do not need the app. Polls are not listed anywhere and are hidden from search engines. Each phone or browser votes once.

    Privacy. No accounts and no ads. The polls you made and the ones you voted on are remembered only on this device. Votes are stored with a random device id that is kept only as a one-way hash.
