Description:
All your timers on one screen: countdowns, stopwatches and alarms side by side, with Pomodoro, interval training and date countdowns one tap away.

* Several countdowns at once, each with its own name ("Pasta", "Tea")
* Stopwatches with laps, fastest and slowest marked
* Alarms: daily or once, snooze, your own spoken message
* Pomodoro: focus and breaks, rounds, auto-advance
* Interval timer: Tabata, HIIT, EMOM and your own work / rest / rounds
* Countdown to a date: birthdays, holidays, trips
* Display mode: big tiles of just the times, great on a counter or a desk
* Live countdown outside the app: in the status bar and notifications on Android, on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island on iPhone
* Alarms ring like a phone alarm even when the app is closed: full screen with Stop and Snooze on Android, the system alarm on iPhone (iOS 26+)
* Turn each of these on or off in Settings
* Light and dark mode, bigger text, keep-the-screen-awake option

Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

Review notes (ASC): No account or sign-in. Start a countdown (e.g. tap 30s, then Start) and leave the app: a Live Activity shows the countdown and an alert fires when it ends. On iOS 26+ the first start asks for AlarmKit permission so countdowns and alarms ring like the Clock app; otherwise a time-sensitive local notification is used. Nothing is sent over the network.

Play declarations: Exact alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM) and full-screen intents (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT) are used because the app's core function is an alarm clock and timer: user-set countdowns and alarms must ring on time and show a full-screen Stop / Snooze screen when the phone is locked.
