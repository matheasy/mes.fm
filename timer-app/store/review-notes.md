1. Purpose and audience
MES Timer puts countdowns, stopwatches and alarms on one screen, side by side, with Pomodoro, interval training (Tabata / HIIT) and countdowns to a date one tap away. It is for anyone who needs more than one timer at a time: cooking several dishes, studying with Pomodoro, workouts, classrooms and offices. The problem it solves: the built-in Clock app runs one timer at a time and has no Pomodoro or interval training.

2. How to use the main features (no account, no sign-in, no sample files needed)
- Countdown: tap a preset (for example 30s) and Start. Tap "Add another timer" for more, each with its own name.
- Stopwatch: Start, then Lap; the fastest and slowest laps are marked.
- Alarm: set a time, optional label and spoken message, then switch it on.
- More timers: the "Show:" row at the top adds Pomodoro, Interval Timer and Countdown to a Date.
- Display: big tiles showing only the times.
- Outside the app: while a countdown runs, a Live Activity shows it on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. When it ends, an alert fires even if the app is closed. On iOS 26 and later the first Start asks for AlarmKit permission, so countdowns and alarms ring like the Clock app. On earlier versions a time-sensitive local notification is used instead.
- Settings (Sound and options): turn the live countdown, the alerts and "ring like an alarm" on or off.

3. External services
None. The app makes no network requests for its features: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no payments, no AI services. Everything is stored on the device.

4. Regional differences
None. The app works the same in every region.
